# Layer 1 — Mission

**BICM Version:** 1.0.0  
**Purpose:** Why we exist  

## Mission Statement

Build the world's best completely free AI education platform that operates:
- Without proprietary APIs
- Without cloud dependency
- Without expensive databases
- Without vector databases
- Without embeddings
- Without internet

## Vision

A child in a remote Himalayan village can learn AI without constant internet. A teacher can teach entirely offline. A contributor can understand any folder without prior conversation. An AI agent can resume work from the filesystem alone.

## Strategic Goals

### Goal 1: Accessibility
- Works offline
- Portable (USB/MicroSD)
- No installation required
- Any text editor can read it

### Goal 2: Quality
- Curriculum aligned with educational standards
- Locally relevant (Himachal Pradesh context)
- Age-appropriate
- Pedagogically sound

### Goal 3: Sustainability
- Open source
- Community-driven
- Version-controlled
- Institutional memory

### Goal 4: Scale
- From 1 village to 1000
- From 1 language to 50
- From 1 age group to all
- From 1 domain to comprehensive
