# YouTube Script: File Management

**Title:** How to Organize Your Computer Files in 5 Minutes
**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Target Audience:** Grade 9 students, beginner computer users
**Style:** Screen recording with voiceover, upbeat and clear

---

## Pre-Production Notes

**Equipment:**
- Screen recording software (OBS Studio, Camtasia, or built-in recorder)
- Microphone for voiceover
- Clean desktop prepared for recording (organized demo folder)

**Preparation:**
- Create a messy desktop with 15+ files for the opening shot
- Prepare an organized folder structure for the demo
- Have sample files ready for naming demonstration
- Test all keyboard shortcuts before recording

---

## Script

### 0:00-0:30 — HOOK

**[VISUAL: Screen recording of a messy desktop with 30+ files scattered everywhere. Files include untitled documents, random screenshots, downloads with default names.]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Does this look familiar? Thirty files on your desktop. No folders. No organization. You know you have a science report in here somewhere, but where? You could spend ten minutes clicking through files... or you could spend five minutes watching this video and never have this problem again."

**[VISUAL: Quick transition — desktop transforms to an organized layout with neat folders and properly named files.]**

"I am going to show you five rules that will completely change how you manage files on your computer. Let's go."

---

### 0:30-1:30 — THE PROBLEM

**[VISUAL: Timer on screen showing 30 minutes. Text overlay: "Average student wastes 30 minutes per week searching for files."]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Here is a number that might surprise you. The average student wastes thirty minutes every single week just looking for files on their computer. That adds up to eighteen hours per school year. Eighteen hours. That is an entire school day lost because files are named badly and nobody knows where anything is."

**[VISUAL: Show examples of bad file names on screen.]**
- untitled.docx
- doc1.pdf
- my homework week 3.docx
- IMG_4521.jpg
- FINAL FINAL presentation.pptx

"And it gets worse. When your files are named things like 'untitled' and 'doc1,' even search cannot help you. You might as well throw your work into a black hole."

**[VISUAL: Transition to clean screen with the 5 rules appearing as a list.]**

"Five rules. Five minutes. Let's fix this."

---

### 1:30-2:30 — FILE NAMING RULES

**[VISUAL: Screen shows the 5 rules as a numbered list, each highlighting as the voiceover mentions it.]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Rule number one: Be descriptive. Your file name should tell you what is inside without opening it."

**[VISUAL: Side-by-side comparison.]**
- Bad: `doc1.txt` ❌
- Good: `science-lab-report.pdf` ✅

"Rule number two: Date first. Use the format year-month-day."

**[VISUAL: Side-by-side comparison.]**
- Bad: `report-aug-5.docx` ❌
- Good: `2026-08-05_report.docx` ✅

"This makes files sort automatically in chronological order. Your most recent work always appears at the top."

"Rule number three: No spaces. Use hyphens instead."

**[VISUAL: Side-by-side comparison.]**
- Bad: `my science report.docx` ❌
- Good: `my-science-report.docx` ✅

"Spaces cause errors on some systems. Hyphens work everywhere."

"Rule number four: No special characters."

**[VISUAL: Show forbidden characters: / \ : * ? " < > |]**

"These characters have special meanings in computers. Avoid them."

"Rule number five: Short but clear. Three to eight words."

**[VISUAL: Side-by-side comparison.]**
- Bad: `my really long presentation that I worked on for hours.pptx` ❌
- Good: `science-presentation-final.pptx` ✅

"Long names get cut off and are hard to read."

---

### 2:30-3:30 — FOLDER ORGANIZATION

**[VISUAL: Screen shows a folder tree being created step by step.]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Now let's talk about folders. Files without folders are like books thrown on the floor. You might find what you need, but it takes forever."

**[VISUAL: Show three methods as separate diagrams.]**

"There are three ways to organize folders."

**Method 1: By Subject**
```
School/
├── Science/
├── English/
└── Math/
```

**Method 2: By Project**
```
Projects/
├── Science-Fair/
├── English-Essay/
└── History-Presentation/
```

**Method 3: By Date**
```
2026/
├── August/
│   ├── Week-1/
│   └── Week-2/
└── September/
```

**[VISUAL: Live demo — create folders on screen using file explorer.]**

"Let me show you. I am going to create a folder structure for my schoolwork right now."

**[VISUAL: Screen recording of creating folders, subfolders, navigating the hierarchy.]**

"Notice how each folder has a clear purpose. I always know where to put a new file and where to find an old one."

"The golden rule: create folders before you need them. A moment of organization now saves hours of searching later."

---

### 3:30-4:15 — BACKUP BASICS

**[VISUAL: Diagram showing the 3-2-1 backup rule.]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"The last key topic: backups. Your files are valuable. Hard drive failure, stolen laptop, accidental deletion — these things happen. The backup rule is simple."

**[VISUAL: Three circles appearing one by one.]**

"The three-two-one rule. Keep three copies of important files. Use two different types of storage. And keep one copy offsite, like in the cloud."

**[VISUAL: Show Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud logos with free storage amounts.]**

"Google Drive gives you fifteen gigabytes free. OneDrive gives you five. iCloud gives you five. These are more than enough for school files."

**[VISUAL: Quick demo of uploading a file to Google Drive.]**

"It takes thirty seconds to upload a file. Thirty seconds to protect your work forever."

---

### 4:15-5:00 — CALL TO ACTION

**[VISUAL: Checklist appearing on screen with each item being checked off.]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Here is your challenge. Do these five things today."

"One. Rename the three most important files on your desktop using proper naming conventions."

"Two. Create a folder structure for your schoolwork."

"Three. Move files from your desktop into the correct folders."

"Four. Delete files you no longer need from Downloads and the Recycle Bin."

"Five. Upload your most important files to a cloud storage service."

**[VISUAL: Before and after desktop comparison.]**

"That is it. Five actions. Twenty minutes. And your digital life is organized."

"If this video helped you, hit subscribe. Share it with a friend who has a messy desktop. And leave a comment telling me your best file management tip."

**[VISUAL: Bhavya Foundation logo with subscribe button animation.]**

"I will see you in the next one."
