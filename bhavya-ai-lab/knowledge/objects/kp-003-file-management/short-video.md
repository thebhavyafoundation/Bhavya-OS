# Short Video Script: File Management

**Title:** 5 Rules to Never Lose a File Again
**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Platform:** Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / TikTok
**Target Audience:** Students aged 13-18
**Style:** Fast-paced, text overlays, screen recording demos

---

## Script

### 0:00-0:05 — Hook

**[VISUAL: Quick cuts of messy desktop, frustrated student, files named "untitled" and "doc1"]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Stop naming your files 'untitled'"

**VOICEOVER:** "Stop naming your files 'untitled' — here is how to never lose a file again."

---

### 0:05-0:15 — Rule 1 and 2

**[VISUAL: Screen recording — rename a file from "untitled.docx" to "2026-08-05_science-report.pdf"]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Rule 1: Date first — YYYY-MM-DD"

**VOICEOVER:** "Rule one: date first. Use year-month-day. Files sort automatically."

**[VISUAL: Show file list sorting correctly]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Rule 2: Be descriptive"

**VOICEOVER:** "Rule two: be descriptive. 'science-report' not 'doc1.'"

---

### 0:15-0:30 — Rules 3 and 4

**[VISUAL: Screen recording — rename "my file.docx" to "my-file.docx"]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Rule 3: No spaces — use hyphens"

**VOICEOVER:** "Rule three: no spaces. Use hyphens instead. Spaces cause errors."

**[VISUAL: Show forbidden characters: / \ : * ? " < > |]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Rule 4: No special characters"

**VOICEOVER:** "Rule four: no special characters. Slash, colon, asterisk — avoid them all."

---

### 0:30-0:45 — Rule 5 and Folder Organization

**[VISUAL: Screen recording — rename "my really long presentation.pptx" to "science-presentation-final.pptx"]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Rule 5: Short but clear — 3-8 words"

**VOICEOVER:** "Rule five: short but clear. Three to eight words."

**[VISUAL: Screen recording — create folder structure: School/Science/Lab-Reports]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Organize with folders"

**VOICEOVER:** "Organize with folders. By subject, by project, or by date."

---

### 0:45-0:55 — Backup and Call to Action

**[VISUAL: Show 3-2-1 diagram: 3 copies, 2 types, 1 offsite]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "3-2-1 Backup Rule"

**VOICEOVER:** "Back up your work. Three copies, two storage types, one in the cloud."

**[VISUAL: Show uploading a file to Google Drive]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Free: Google Drive (15 GB)"

**VOICEOVER:** "Google Drive gives you fifteen gigs free. Use it."

---

### 0:55-1:00 — Closing

**[VISUAL: Before and after desktop comparison — messy vs. organized]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "Apply these rules today"

**VOICEOVER:** "Save this video. Apply these rules today. Your future self will thank you."

**[VISUAL: Bhavya Foundation logo]**

**TEXT OVERLAY:** "@BhavyaFoundation"

## Production Notes

**Pacing:**
- Each rule gets 5-7 seconds
- Fast cuts between examples
- Text appears on screen before voiceover mentions it
- Music: upbeat electronic, low volume under voiceover

**Text Overlay Style:**
- White text with dark background for readability
- Bold, sans-serif font (Montserrat or similar)
- Appears at bottom third of screen
- Rule numbers highlighted in brand color (orange or teal)

**Visual Elements:**
- Screen recordings for all file operations
- Side-by-side comparisons (bad → good)
- Icons for each rule (calendar, hyphen, warning, etc.)
- Bhavya Foundation logo watermark in corner

**Audio:**
- Clear voiceover (record in quiet environment)
- Background music: royalty-free, upbeat, minimal
- Sound effects: subtle whoosh for transitions, click for file operations
