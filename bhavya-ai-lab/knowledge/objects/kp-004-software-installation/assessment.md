## Section A: Scenario-Based Multiple Choice (5 questions, 2 points each)

### Question 1

Your friend wants to download a video editor. They found a website called "free-video-editor-download.com" that offers a popular paid video editor for free. What should your friend do?

- A) Download it — free software is always safe
- B) Check if the site has positive reviews, then download
- C) Go to the official website of the video editor and download from there instead
- D) Ask someone who is good with computers to download it for them

**Correct Answer:** C
**Concept:** Safe Download Sources
**Explanation:** Third-party download sites, even with good reviews, may modify installers to include malware. The official developer's website is the only guaranteed safe source.

---

### Question 2

You are installing a text editor. During installation, you see this screen: "Install ABC Toolbar and set ABC Search as your default search engine. [Pre-checked]" What should you do?

- A) Leave it checked — the installer recommends it
- B) Uncheck it — you did not ask for this software
- C) Click "Express Install" to skip this screen
- D) Accept it — it will not harm your computer

**Correct Answer:** B
**Concept:** Installation Practices
**Explanation:** Pre-checked bundled software is designed to be installed without your consent. Always uncheck offers you did not explicitly seek. Choose "Custom" installation over "Express" to see all options.

---

### Question 3

Your classmate installed a note-taking app. The app immediately asks for permission to access the camera, microphone, and contacts. Your classmate asks you: "Should I allow all of these?" What should you tell them?

- A) Allow all — the app knows what it needs
- B) Allow camera and microphone but deny contacts
- C) Deny all — a note-taking app does not need any of these
- D) Allow camera but deny microphone and contacts

**Correct Answer:** C
**Concept:** Permissions and Privacy
**Explanation:** The principle of least privilege says software should only have access to what it needs. A note-taking app saves text. It has no legitimate need for camera, microphone, or contacts access.

---

### Question 4

You see a pop-up on a website: "Your computer has 15 viruses! Download our free antivirus now to remove them!" What is this most likely?

- A) A legitimate security warning from your operating system
- B) A scareware tactic to trick you into downloading malware
- C) A helpful notification from your antivirus software
- D) A system update notification

**Correct Answer:** B
**Concept:** Safe Download Sources
**Explanation:** Legitimate security warnings come from your operating system or installed antivirus, not from browser pop-ups. These pop-ups are scareware — they try to frighten you into downloading harmful software.

---

### Question 5

After installing a new application, you notice your computer is running slower and your browser has a new toolbar you did not install. What most likely happened?

- A) The new application is too heavy for your computer
- B) The installation included bundled software you did not notice
- C) Your computer needs more RAM
- D) The application is a virus

**Correct Answer:** B
**Concept:** Installation Practices
**Explanation:** Bundled software — unwanted programs included with installers — is a common cause of browser toolbars and system slowdowns. This is why reading installation prompts and unchecking pre-selected options is critical.
