## Core Identity Statement

Bhavya AI Lab is a free, offline-first, portable educational operating system for teaching AI, Computer Science, and related fields to children in rural Himachal Pradesh and beyond.

## Identity Principles

1. **Knowledge is the center.** AI is a runtime, not the core.
2. **The filesystem is the operating system.** No hidden state.
3. **Everything is version-controlled.** Not just code — lessons, animations, voice, decisions.
4. **Everything has a schema.** Nothing is "just markdown."
5. **Factories transform knowledge.** They don't create it.
6. **The institution outlives any AI model.** If OpenAI disappears, we still function.
