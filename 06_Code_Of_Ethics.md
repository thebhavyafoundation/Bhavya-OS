DOCUMENT 6
BHAVYA FOUNDATION
CODE OF ETHICS
Version 1.0

PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation exists because society places its trust in us.
That trust is earned not only through successful projects, but through the character, integrity, and conduct of every person associated with the Foundation.
This Code of Ethics establishes the standards expected of every Trustee, office-bearer, employee, volunteer, consultant, advisor, intern, and representative of Bhavya Foundation.
Every individual shall uphold both the letter and the spirit of this Code.
Ethics shall never be sacrificed for convenience, popularity, financial gain, or institutional growth.

ARTICLE 1
OUR ETHICAL FOUNDATION
Every action taken by Bhavya Foundation shall reflect:
Integrity
Honesty
Respect
Accountability
Stewardship
Transparency
Professionalism
Humility
Compassion
Responsibility
These values are not optional.
They define the identity of the Foundation.

ARTICLE 2
DUTY TO THE PUBLIC
Every representative shall remember:
The Foundation exists for public benefit.
Not for personal benefit.
Every decision must strengthen public trust.
Every action should improve the lives of the communities we serve.

ARTICLE 3
PERSONAL CONDUCT
Every representative shall:
Act honestly.
Speak truthfully.
Treat everyone with dignity.
Maintain professionalism.
Avoid abusive behaviour.
Avoid discrimination prohibited by law.
Respect diversity.
Protect the Foundation's reputation.
Represent the Foundation responsibly in public and online.

ARTICLE 4
CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Every representative shall avoid situations where personal interests conflict with the interests of the Foundation.
Examples include:
Awarding contracts to oneself or close relatives without proper procedures.
Using Foundation opportunities for personal business.
Accepting benefits that could improperly influence decisions.
Participating in decisions where one has a material personal interest.
Potential conflicts shall be disclosed promptly.
The Foundation values transparency over concealment.

ARTICLE 5
FINANCIAL INTEGRITY
Every person handling Foundation resources shall:
Use funds only for authorized purposes.
Maintain accurate records.
Protect Foundation assets.
Avoid waste.
Prevent fraud.
Report suspected misuse.
No individual shall use Foundation property for personal benefit unless expressly authorized by policy.

ARTICLE 6
GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
Representatives shall not accept gifts, hospitality, or favors that could improperly influence—or reasonably appear to influence—their judgment.
Modest tokens of appreciation may be accepted where appropriate and lawful, provided they do not create an actual or perceived conflict of interest and are handled in accordance with the Foundation's Gift Policy.

ARTICLE 7
RESPECT FOR PEOPLE
Every individual shall be treated with dignity.
Harassment.
Bullying.
Threats.
Intimidation.
Humiliation.
Discrimination.
Retaliation.
are inconsistent with the values of Bhavya Foundation.
The Foundation shall maintain a respectful workplace and volunteer environment.

ARTICLE 8
CHILD SAFETY
The Foundation adopts a zero-tolerance approach to abuse, exploitation, neglect, or harassment of children.
Every person working with children shall:
Follow safeguarding procedures.
Maintain professional boundaries.
Respect parental rights.
Immediately report concerns through established channels.
The safety of children takes precedence over institutional reputation.

ARTICLE 9
ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY
Every representative shall strive to:
Reduce waste.
Protect biodiversity.
Respect wildlife.
Conserve water.
Use resources responsibly.
Promote sustainable practices.
Environmental stewardship begins within the Foundation itself.

ARTICLE 10
USE OF TECHNOLOGY
Technology shall be used responsibly.
Representatives shall:
Protect personal data.
Respect privacy.
Use AI ethically.
Avoid spreading misinformation.
Protect digital assets.
Respect intellectual property.
Use Foundation technology only for authorized purposes.

ARTICLE 11
CONFIDENTIALITY
Representatives shall protect confidential information concerning:
Beneficiaries.
Children.
Donors.
Employees.
Volunteers.
Partners.
Legal matters.
Financial information.
Confidentiality continues even after leaving the Foundation unless disclosure is required by law.

ARTICLE 12
PUBLIC COMMUNICATION
Only authorized representatives may speak on behalf of the Foundation regarding official matters.
All public communications shall be:
Truthful.
Accurate.
Respectful.
Evidence-based.
Free from misleading claims.
Personal opinions shall not be presented as official positions of the Foundation.

ARTICLE 13
POLITICAL NEUTRALITY
Bhavya Foundation is a public charitable institution.
The Foundation shall not be used to campaign for or against political parties or candidates.
Representatives retain their personal rights as citizens but shall clearly distinguish personal views from official Foundation positions.

ARTICLE 14
SOCIAL MEDIA
Representatives shall use social media responsibly.
They shall not:
Disclose confidential information.
Harass individuals.
Spread misinformation.
Misrepresent Foundation activities.
Damage the Foundation's reputation.
When identifying themselves with the Foundation online, they should communicate respectfully and professionally.

ARTICLE 15
REPORTING ETHICAL CONCERNS
Every representative has a responsibility to report genuine concerns regarding:
Fraud.
Corruption.
Harassment.
Child safety.
Financial misconduct.
Abuse of authority.
Environmental violations.
Serious policy breaches.
Reports made honestly and in good faith shall be handled fairly and confidentially.
Retaliation against individuals who raise genuine concerns in good faith is prohibited.

ARTICLE 16
CONSEQUENCES OF ETHICAL MISCONDUCT
Violations of this Code may result in actions including:
counseling or additional training;
written warning;
suspension of duties;
removal from office or volunteer role, following applicable procedures;
referral to law enforcement or regulatory authorities where required by law.
The Foundation shall ensure fair procedures before disciplinary decisions are made.

ARTICLE 17
LEADERSHIP BY EXAMPLE
The Founder, Trustees, and senior leadership bear a special responsibility.
They shall model the conduct expected throughout the Foundation.
Ethical leadership is demonstrated through consistent action rather than position or authority.

ARTICLE 18
ETHICAL DECISION FRAMEWORK
Before making an important decision, every representative should ask:
Is it lawful?
Is it consistent with our Constitution?
Does it advance the public good?
Would I be comfortable if this decision were publicly reported?
Would this strengthen or weaken public trust?
Would I make the same decision if I received no recognition for it?
If any answer raises significant concern, the decision should be reconsidered or discussed with appropriate leadership.

ARTICLE 19
ETHICAL PLEDGE
Every representative shall affirm:
"I commit myself to serving Bhavya Foundation with honesty, integrity, humility, and respect. I shall protect the Foundation's mission, uphold this Code of Ethics, comply with applicable laws and policies, respect every individual, safeguard the Foundation's resources, and act in a manner worthy of the public trust placed in this institution."

APPENDIX A
The Ten Ethical Commitments
We serve the public before ourselves.
We protect nature responsibly.
We expand knowledge ethically.
We preserve heritage respectfully.
We tell the truth.
We manage resources responsibly.
We respect every individual.
We remain transparent and accountable.
We protect children and vulnerable persons.
We leave Bhavya Foundation stronger than we found it.

Closing Declaration
"The reputation of Bhavya Foundation will never be built solely by its projects, but by the integrity with which every person associated with it serves the public."
