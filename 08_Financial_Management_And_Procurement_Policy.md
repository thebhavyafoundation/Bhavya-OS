PREAMBLE
The financial resources of Bhavya Foundation are held in trust for the benefit of the public.
Every rupee entrusted to the Foundation represents the confidence of donors, the hopes of beneficiaries, and the responsibility of the institution.
The purpose of this Policy is to establish robust financial management practices that ensure accountability, transparency, lawful compliance, prudent stewardship, and long-term sustainability.
This Policy shall be interpreted consistently with the Constitution, Trust Deed, Governance Manual, and applicable law.

ARTICLE 1
OBJECTIVES
This Policy seeks to:
safeguard Foundation assets;
maintain accurate financial records;
ensure responsible budgeting;
establish internal controls;
promote transparent procurement;
prevent fraud and misuse;
support informed decision-making; and
strengthen public confidence.

ARTICLE 2
FINANCIAL PRINCIPLES
All financial decisions shall be guided by the following principles:
Public Benefit – Resources shall be used only to advance the Foundation's charitable purposes.
Stewardship – Every expenditure shall represent prudent use of charitable funds.
Transparency – Financial information shall be recorded accurately and reported honestly.
Accountability – Authority over finances shall always be accompanied by responsibility.
Sustainability – Financial planning shall support the Foundation's long-term resilience.

ARTICLE 3
ANNUAL BUDGET
The Foundation shall prepare an Annual Budget before the commencement of each financial year.
The Budget shall include:
projected income;
programme expenditure;
administrative expenditure;
capital expenditure;
reserve allocation;
contingency provisions; and
major project investments.
The Board shall review and approve the Budget before implementation.

ARTICLE 4
BANKING
All Foundation funds shall be deposited into bank accounts opened in the name of Bhavya Foundation.
The Board shall authorize signatories by resolution.
Bank accounts shall not be maintained in the personal name of any Trustee, employee, or volunteer.
Electronic banking credentials shall be protected through appropriate security measures.

ARTICLE 5
RECEIPTS & DONATIONS
Every donation shall be:
acknowledged promptly;
recorded accurately;
deposited without unreasonable delay; and
used consistently with donor restrictions where applicable.
Official receipts shall include:
receipt number;
date;
donor name (where available);
amount or description of donation;
mode of payment;
authorized signature or digital approval.
Anonymous donations shall be handled in accordance with applicable law.

ARTICLE 6
EXPENDITURE
No expenditure shall be incurred unless:
it supports the Foundation's objectives;
sufficient budget is available;
the required approvals have been obtained; and
supporting documentation is retained.
Expenditures shall be reasonable, necessary, and proportionate.


ARTICLE 8
PROCUREMENT
Procurement shall be:
fair;
transparent;
competitive where appropriate;
based on value for money;
free from improper influence.
For significant purchases, the Foundation should ordinarily seek multiple quotations unless circumstances reasonably justify another approach.
Procurement records shall document:
requirement;
quotations received;
evaluation;
selected supplier;
approval;
contract (where applicable).

ARTICLE 9
ASSET MANAGEMENT
The Foundation shall maintain an Asset Register including:
computers;
furniture;
vehicles;
books;
laboratory equipment;
digital assets;
project equipment; and
other significant property.
Each asset shall have:
Asset ID;
description;
purchase date;
location;
custodian;
purchase value;
condition; and
disposal record (if applicable).
An annual physical verification of significant assets should be conducted.

ARTICLE 10
CASH MANAGEMENT
The Foundation shall minimize cash transactions wherever practicable.
Where cash handling is necessary:
cash receipts shall be recorded immediately;
cash shall be secured appropriately;
periodic reconciliation shall be performed;
supporting documentation shall be maintained.

ARTICLE 11
REIMBURSEMENTS
Reimbursements shall require:
prior authorization where practicable;
original supporting documents;
explanation of purpose;
verification by an independent approver.
Individuals shall not approve reimbursement claims submitted by themselves.

ARTICLE 12
TRAVEL & HOSPITALITY
Reasonable travel and hospitality expenses incurred for Foundation purposes may be reimbursed in accordance with Board-approved guidelines.
Luxury or excessive expenditure is inconsistent with the Foundation's stewardship obligations.

ARTICLE 13
ACCOUNTING RECORDS
The Foundation shall maintain complete and accurate accounting records.
Records shall include:
cash book;
bank book;
general ledger;
fixed asset register;
donor register;
grant register;
procurement records;
invoices;
receipts;
contracts;
payroll records (if applicable).
Records shall be retained in accordance with law and the Foundation's Record Retention Policy.

ARTICLE 14
INTERNAL CONTROLS
The Foundation shall maintain internal controls, including:
segregation of duties where practicable;
authorization procedures;
periodic reconciliations;
inventory controls;
password protection for financial systems;
document retention;
fraud prevention measures.
No individual should exercise unrestricted control over all stages of a financial transaction.

ARTICLE 15
AUDIT
The Foundation shall comply with all statutory audit requirements.
The Board may also commission internal reviews or independent audits where appropriate.
Audit findings shall be considered promptly, and corrective actions shall be documented and monitored.

ARTICLE 16
FRAUD PREVENTION
Any suspected fraud, theft, misappropriation, or financial irregularity shall be reported immediately.
The Foundation shall investigate credible allegations fairly and confidentially.
Where required, matters shall be referred to the appropriate authorities.

ARTICLE 17
RESERVES
The Foundation may establish financial reserves to support:
continuity of operations;
emergencies;
strategic initiatives;
maintenance of assets; and
long-term sustainability.
The Board shall periodically review reserve levels.

ARTICLE 18
FINANCIAL REPORTING
Management shall provide the Board with periodic financial reports, including:
budget versus actual expenditure;
cash position;
significant variances;
major commitments;
grant utilization;
reserve position.
Reports should be clear, accurate, and timely.

ARTICLE 19
RECORDS & DOCUMENT RETENTION
Financial records shall be retained securely for the period required by law and Foundation policy.
Electronic records shall be backed up regularly.
Confidential financial information shall be protected from unauthorized access.

ARTICLE 20
POLICY REVIEW
This Policy shall be reviewed every two years or earlier where:
legal requirements change;
governance practices evolve; or
the Board determines revision is appropriate.

