# Ecological Stewardship & Forest GIS

The Nature Initiative protects critical forest corridors, restores degraded ecosystems, and monitors biodiversity using real-time satellite GIS telemetry.

## Key Active Programs

### Western Ghats Canopy Restoration
Monitoring 14,200 hectares of primary canopy rainforest, deploying automated sensor nodes to track humidity, temperature, and illegal deforestation alerts.

### Sacred Grove Preservation Network
Partnering with local communities to map, fence, and ecologically restore ancient indigenous sacred groves containing rare endemic flora.

### Automated Wildlife Corridor Telemetry
Using non-invasive AI camera traps and bio-acoustic sensors to track tiger, elephant, and endemic bird migration corridors.
