## Nature

Protecting and restoring forests, biodiversity corridors, and natural ecosystems. Bhavya Foundation operates forest monitoring, conservation programs, and environmental research that runs on real-time GIS data.

## Knowledge

Advancing education, research, and open access to institutional knowledge. Every research output, governance document, and institutional decision is archived and made publicly accessible.

## Heritage

Preserving cultural history, traditional knowledge, and community heritage. We digitize, document, and protect heritage artifacts, oral traditions, and historical records.

## Community

Empowering volunteers, donors, and citizens to participate in the Foundation's mission. We build transparent systems where everyone can see what is being done with their trust and resources.
