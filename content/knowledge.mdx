# Institutional Knowledge Platform

Knowledge belongs to everyone. The Foundation maintains an open, machine-readable repository of technical standards, scientific research papers, educational curricula, and governance records.

## Core Pillars

### Open Research Archives
All scientific publications, environmental assessments, and platform engineering specifications are released under open licenses.

### Interactive Knowledge Graph
Every document, decision, standard, and release is linked in a bidirectional knowledge graph (`registry/knowledge-graph.json`) accessible to humans and AI agents.

### Digital Educational Resources
Free, multilingual learning modules covering ecology, sustainable agriculture, indigenous heritage, and open-source software engineering.
