# Preserving History for Future Generations

The Heritage Initiative documents, digitizes, and safeguards indigenous traditions, historical manuscripts, oral histories, and ancient architecture.

## Active Projects

### Manuscript Digitization Vault
High-resolution 3D and multispectral scanning of fragile palm-leaf and paper manuscripts, making rare historical texts accessible worldwide.

### Oral History Repository
Recording and preserving stories, folk songs, and ecological wisdom from elder community members across regional dialects.

### Temple & Monument Architectural Preservation
Photogrammetry and laser scanning of historic monuments to create digital twins for heritage preservation and structural monitoring.
