# Empowering Grassroots Action

The Foundation is powered by thousands of volunteers, researchers, conservationists, and citizens working together to protect nature and heritage.

## Get Involved

### Field Volunteer Corps
Participate in tree planting drives, sacred grove fencing, wildlife census surveys, and community education workshops.

### Technical & Digital Volunteers
Contribute code, verify links, translate documentation, or assist in manuscript transcription from anywhere in the world.

### Transparent Donations
100% of public contributions directly fund field projects. Track exactly where every donation is allocated on our live Transparency Portal.
