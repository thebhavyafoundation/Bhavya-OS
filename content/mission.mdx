# Built for the Long Term

Bhavya Foundation was established with a singular governing principle: to create an enduring institutional foundation that serves humanity and the natural world across generations.

## Governing Principles

### 1. Radical Transparency
Every institutional decision, architectural standard, financial flow, and operational policy is documented in version-controlled repositories and made publicly verifiable in real time.

### 2. Provider & Vendor Agnosticism
We build on open-source standards and abstract interfaces, ensuring that institutional operations never depend on any single commercial vendor or proprietary platform.

### 3. Institutional Memory
Decisions are never lost or modified retroactively. Every Architecture Decision Record (ADR) and policy is immutable, forming an unalterable history of institutional evolution.

### 4. Direct Impact
Resources are routed directly to frontline conservation, education access, digital preservation, and community empowerment with zero unnecessary administrative overhead.
