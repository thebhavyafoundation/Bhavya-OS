RECITALS
WHEREAS the Settlor, being inspired by the ideals of public service, environmental stewardship, education, cultural preservation, and community development, has resolved to establish a Public Charitable Trust under the name Bhavya Foundation.
AND WHEREAS the Settlor has expressed the intention that the Trust shall function solely for charitable purposes and for the benefit of the public without distinction.
AND WHEREAS the Trustees have agreed to act as the first Trustees of the Trust and to administer the Trust in accordance with this Deed, applicable laws, and the charitable objects herein contained.
NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

ARTICLE 3
NATURE OF TRUST
The Trust is established as an irrevocable Public Charitable Trust.
The Trust shall exist exclusively for charitable purposes.
No part of its income or property shall be distributed to the Settlor, Trustees, or any private individual except as permitted by law for legitimate charitable administration.

ARTICLE 7
POWERS OF TRUSTEES
Subject to applicable law, the Trustees may:
Acquire property.
Sell or lease property for charitable purposes.
Open bank accounts.
Employ staff.
Appoint committees.
Establish institutions.
Accept donations.
Enter contracts.
Invest Trust funds in accordance with law.
Institute or defend legal proceedings.
Maintain accounts.
Undertake all lawful acts necessary to fulfil the Trust's charitable objects.

ARTICLE 8
MANAGING TRUSTEE
The Settlor shall serve as the first Managing Trustee.
The Managing Trustee shall:
Supervise administration.
Implement Trustee decisions.
Represent the Trust.
Execute authorized agreements.
Coordinate programmes.
Maintain records.
Ensure compliance.

ARTICLE 9
MEETINGS
The Trustees shall meet at least four times annually.
Proper notice shall be given.
Minutes shall be recorded.
Decisions should preferably be taken by consensus.
Where voting is necessary, the procedure shall be governed by this Deed and any adopted bylaws.

ARTICLE 10
BANK ACCOUNTS
The Trust shall maintain accounts in its own name.
Bank operations shall be conducted only through authorized signatories approved by the Trustees.

ARTICLE 11
ACCOUNTS
Proper books of account shall be maintained.
Accounts shall be audited where required by law.

ARTICLE 12
DONATIONS
The Trust may accept donations in cash, kind, property, grants, and lawful contributions.
The Trustees reserve the right to decline any donation inconsistent with the charitable purposes or independence of the Trust.

ARTICLE 13
IRREVOCABILITY
The Trust is irrevocable.
No Trustee or the Settlor shall have any beneficial ownership over the Trust property.

ARTICLE 14
DISSOLUTION
If the Trust is dissolved in accordance with applicable law, all remaining assets, after satisfaction of lawful liabilities, shall be transferred to another public charitable institution having similar charitable objects.
Under no circumstances shall Trust assets be distributed among the Settlor, Trustees, or private individuals.

ARTICLE 15
GOVERNING LAW
This Trust shall be governed by the laws applicable to Public Charitable Trusts in India and the State in which it is registered.
