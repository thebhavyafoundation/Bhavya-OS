PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation believes that knowledge is one of humanity's greatest public resources.
Access to knowledge should not be determined by geography, income, or social background.
The Bhavya Digital Library is established to provide equitable access to books, research, digital resources, artificial intelligence tools, educational technology, and lifelong learning opportunities.
The Library shall serve as a public institution dedicated to education, innovation, research, and community development.

ARTICLE 1
PURPOSE
The Digital Library shall:
Expand access to knowledge.
Reduce educational inequality.
Promote AI literacy.
Encourage research.
Preserve knowledge.
Support lifelong learning.
Build digitally empowered communities.

ARTICLE 2

Licensed Educational Platforms

ARTICLE 14

APPENDIX B
Library User Pledge
"I shall use the Bhavya Digital Library responsibly, respect knowledge and intellectual property, protect Library resources, use Artificial Intelligence ethically, support fellow learners, and contribute to a culture of curiosity, lifelong learning, and public service."

APPENDIX C
Vision 2040 – Bhavya Knowledge Network
The Foundation shall strive to build an interconnected network of community knowledge centres across rural and urban India.
Each centre should integrate:
Digital Library
AI Lab
Computer Education
Environmental Learning
Career Development
Research Resources
Community Innovation Space
The long-term objective is to create self-sustaining learning ecosystems that empower individuals, strengthen communities, and expand access to knowledge for generations to come.

Closing Declaration
"A library is not defined by the number of books on its shelves, but by the number of minds it empowers. The Bhavya Digital Library shall exist to ensure that every learner, regardless of background, has the opportunity to access knowledge, embrace innovation, and contribute meaningfully to society."

Founder's Knowledge Principle
"Knowledge should travel farther than roads, faster than technology, and deeper than classrooms. Every Bhavya Digital Library shall be a place where curiosity is welcomed, ideas are shared, and every individual is given the opportunity to learn, create, and lead."
