PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation is committed to creating safe, respectful, and inclusive environments for every child who participates in its programmes.
The Foundation believes that every child has the right to protection from abuse, neglect, exploitation, discrimination, harassment, and any form of harm.
Safeguarding is a shared responsibility. Every Trustee, employee, volunteer, consultant, trainer, and partner must place the welfare of children above all other interests.
No programme objective, institutional reputation, or individual relationship shall ever take precedence over the safety and dignity of a child.

ARTICLE 1
PURPOSE
This Policy aims to:
Protect every child participating in Foundation programmes.
Prevent abuse, neglect, exploitation, and harassment.
Establish clear behavioural standards.
Define reporting procedures.
Promote a culture of safeguarding.
Comply with applicable child protection laws.

ARTICLE 3
GUIDING PRINCIPLES
Every child shall:
Be respected.
Be protected.
Be heard.
Be treated fairly.
Be free from violence.
Be free from exploitation.
Be free from discrimination.
Be safe.

ARTICLE 4
DEFINITION OF A CHILD
For the purposes of this Policy, a child means any individual below the age recognized by applicable law as a minor.

ARTICLE 5
ZERO TOLERANCE
Bhavya Foundation adopts a Zero Tolerance Policy toward:
Physical Abuse
Sexual Abuse
Emotional Abuse
Psychological Abuse
Neglect
Bullying
Harassment
Child Labour
Exploitation
Online Grooming
Inappropriate Communication
Retaliation against a child who raises concerns
Any confirmed breach shall result in immediate action consistent with applicable law and Foundation procedures.

ARTICLE 6
EXPECTED CONDUCT
Every representative shall:
Treat every child equally.
Use respectful language.
Maintain professional boundaries.
Encourage participation.
Protect privacy.
Follow school rules.
Obtain necessary permissions before activities.
Immediately report safeguarding concerns.

ARTICLE 7
PROHIBITED CONDUCT
No representative shall:
Be alone with a child in a closed or isolated space unless authorized and appropriate safeguards are in place.
Use physical punishment.
Use abusive language.
Ridicule or humiliate a child.
Share inappropriate content.
Exchange personal gifts without authorization.
Request personal favours.
Develop inappropriate personal relationships.
Communicate privately through personal social media or messaging platforms unless authorized for programme purposes and with appropriate oversight.
Transport a child alone in a private vehicle unless there is a lawful emergency or prior authorization and appropriate safeguards.

ARTICLE 8
PHOTOGRAPHY & MEDIA
Photographs, videos, and recordings of children shall only be taken:
for legitimate Foundation purposes;
with the necessary permissions or consents required by law or the partnering institution;
in a manner that protects the dignity and privacy of the child.
Images shall never be manipulated or used in a misleading or exploitative manner.
Personal information shall not be published alongside images unless appropriate consent has been obtained.

ARTICLE 9
DIGITAL SAFEGUARDING
For online programmes:
Official Foundation platforms shall be used.
Sessions should, where practical, include more than one authorized adult or be conducted in collaboration with the partnering institution.
Chats shall remain professional.
Sessions may be recorded where appropriate and lawful.
Personal contact information shall not be requested unless necessary for programme administration and handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Policy.

ARTICLE 10
PARENTAL ENGAGEMENT
Where appropriate, parents or legal guardians shall be informed about:
programme objectives;
schedules;
safety arrangements;
participation requirements; and
contact information.
Consent shall be obtained where required by law or programme design.

ARTICLE 11
SCHOOL PARTNERSHIPS
Every school programme shall operate under a written understanding or approval from the school or competent authority.
The Foundation shall respect the school's safeguarding procedures while maintaining its own standards.
Foundation representatives shall comply with school rules during all activities.

ARTICLE 12
REPORTING CONCERNS
Any person who becomes aware of a safeguarding concern shall report it immediately to the designated safeguarding lead or appropriate Foundation authority.
Reports shall include:
date;
location;
persons involved;
factual description of the concern; and
immediate safety actions taken, if any.
Where required by law, the Foundation shall report concerns to the appropriate authorities.

ARTICLE 13
RESPONSE TO CONCERNS
The Foundation shall:
Take all reports seriously.
Protect the child from further harm.
Maintain confidentiality.
Avoid retaliation.
Conduct or facilitate appropriate review.
Cooperate with lawful investigations.
Support affected individuals within the Foundation's role and responsibilities.

ARTICLE 14
TRAINING
All individuals working regularly with children shall receive appropriate safeguarding orientation.
Training should cover:
recognizing signs of abuse;
professional boundaries;
reporting obligations;
appropriate communication;
online safety; and
the Foundation's safeguarding procedures.

ARTICLE 15
CONFIDENTIALITY
Safeguarding information shall be shared only with those who have a legitimate need to know or where disclosure is required by law.
The privacy and dignity of children shall be respected throughout any safeguarding process.

ARTICLE 16
MONITORING & REVIEW
The Board shall review safeguarding arrangements annually.
Lessons learned from incidents, training, and programme evaluations shall inform improvements to this Policy.


Closing Declaration
"Every child who participates in a Bhavya Foundation programme should return home not only with new knowledge or experiences, but with their dignity, safety, and trust fully protected. Safeguarding is the responsibility of every individual who serves this Foundation."
