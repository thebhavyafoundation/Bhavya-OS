# Workflow: Research Heritage

## Trigger

- Heritage mission research request

## Steps

1. **Define Scope** — Clarify research questions
2. **Gather Data** — Collect cultural information
3. **Analyze** — Process and analyze data
4. **Synthesize** — Create research summary
5. **Document** — Write research report
6. **Review** — Expert review
7. **Publish** — Add to knowledge base

## Agents Involved

- Researcher (data gathering)
- Writer (documentation)
- Historian (knowledge preservation)
- Translator (multilingual support)

## Output

- Research report
- Knowledge base entry
- Cultural documentation
- Updated documentation
