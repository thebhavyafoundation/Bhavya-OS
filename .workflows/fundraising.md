# Workflow: Fundraising

## Trigger

- Fundraising campaign request

## Steps

1. **Plan Campaign** — Define goals and strategy
2. **Create Content** — Develop campaign materials
3. **Launch** — Start fundraising campaign
4. **Monitor** — Track donations and engagement
5. **Report** — Generate progress reports
6. **Close** — Finalize campaign
7. **Thank** — Send thank you communications

## Agents Involved

- Founder (strategy)
- Writer (content creation)
- Donation (donation processing)
- SocialMedia (promotion)

## Output

- Campaign materials
- Donation records
- Progress reports
- Thank you communications
