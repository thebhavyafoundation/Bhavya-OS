# Workflow: Volunteer Onboarding

## Trigger

- New volunteer application

## Steps

1. **Review Application** — Check volunteer application
2. **Approve** — Approve or reject application
3. **Onboard** — Provide onboarding materials
4. **Assign** — Assign to appropriate team
5. **Track** — Track volunteer activities
6. **Recognize** — Recognize contributions

## Agents Involved

- Volunteer (application review)
- Founder (approval)
- Writer (onboarding materials)

## Output

- Approved application
- Onboarding materials
- Team assignment
- Activity tracking
