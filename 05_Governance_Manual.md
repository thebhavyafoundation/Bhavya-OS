PREFACE
The Constitution defines what Bhavya Foundation is.
The Trust Deed establishes its legal existence.
The Governance Manual explains how the Foundation is governed.
This Manual provides the framework for responsible decision-making, accountability, institutional continuity, and operational excellence.
It shall be read together with the Constitution, Trust Deed, Board Charter, and all Foundation policies.

CHAPTER 1
GOVERNANCE PHILOSOPHY
Bhavya Foundation believes that good governance is not bureaucracy.
It is the disciplined practice of protecting public trust.
Governance ensures that every decision:
serves the public benefit;
protects the Foundation's mission;
complies with applicable law;
respects financial stewardship; and
strengthens the institution.
Governance exists to enable responsible action, not to delay it.

CHAPTER 2
GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE
The governance framework shall consist of:
Founder & Steward
│
Board of Trustees
│
Managing Trustee
│
Executive Director (Future)
│
Programme Directors
│
Employees
│
Volunteers
Each level shall understand its responsibilities and accountability.
Authority shall flow downward.
Accountability shall flow upward.

CHAPTER 3
GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES
Every governance decision shall be guided by:
Integrity
Every decision must be ethical.

Transparency
Information should be shared unless confidentiality is legally or ethically required.

Accountability
Authority requires responsibility.

Independence
The Foundation shall remain free from improper influence.

Stewardship
Resources are held in trust for the public.

Sustainability
Every decision should strengthen the Foundation over the long term.

CHAPTER 4
BOARD OPERATIONS
The Board governs.
The Managing Trustee manages.
Committees advise.
Staff execute.
Volunteers implement.
No office shall interfere unnecessarily with the constitutional responsibilities of another.

CHAPTER 7
DECISION MAKING
The Board should seek:
Consensus.
If consensus cannot reasonably be achieved:
Vote.
Major constitutional matters should receive broad support.
Routine operational matters should not require Board approval unless reserved.

CHAPTER 10
COMMITTEES
Standing Committees shall have written Terms of Reference approved by the Board.
Every committee shall:
Meet as required.
Record minutes.
Submit recommendations.
Report to the Board.
Avoid exercising unauthorized governance powers.
Committees exist to assist—not replace—the Board.

CHAPTER 11
DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY
Authority may be delegated.
Responsibility may not.
Every delegation shall be:
Written.
Time-bound where appropriate.
Clearly defined.
Reviewable.
Revocable by the Board.

CHAPTER 16
FINAL PRINCIPLE
Governance shall never become an obstacle to service.
Likewise, enthusiasm for service shall never excuse poor governance.
The Foundation shall pursue excellence by balancing purpose with discipline.
