# Mentor Lifecycle

## Overview

The Mentor Lifecycle defines how mentors contribute to Bhavya Foundation. Mentors are experienced practitioners who guide students, review projects, and contribute to the institution.

## The Lifecycle

```
Recruitment
  ↓ [Identify potential mentors from community]
Training
  ↓ [Complete mentor training program]
Certification
  ↓ [Demonstrate mentoring competency]
Office Hours
  ↓ [Regular student access]
Project Review
  ↓ [Evaluate student projects]
Student Feedback
  ↓ [Guide student growth]
Research
  ↓ [Contribute to institution knowledge]
Promotion
  ↓ [Take on greater responsibilities]
Recognition
  → [Public acknowledgment of contribution]
```

## Phases

### Recruitment

**Source:**

- Active Contributors
- Community Leaders
- Industry Professionals
- Academic Researchers
- Alumni

**Criteria:**

- Deep AI expertise
- Teaching aptitude
- Community standing
- Portfolio of work
- Recommendation from existing mentors

**Process:**

- Self-nomination or community nomination
- Portfolio review
- Interview with existing mentors
- Mission alignment assessment
- Skills assessment

### Training

**Program:**

- Mentor philosophy and values
- Teaching methods
- Project review techniques
- Student guidance approaches
- Communication skills
- Code review practices
- Documentation standards
- Ethics and boundaries

**Duration:**

- 4 weeks (part-time)
- Weekly sessions
- Practice projects
- Peer mentoring

**Outcome:**

- Mentor training certificate
- Practice portfolio
- Peer feedback
- Mentor assignment

### Certification

**Requirements:**

- Complete training program
- Pass mentor assessment
- Complete practice mentoring
- Receive peer recommendation
- Demonstrate teaching competency

**Assessment:**

- Portfolio review
- Teaching demonstration
- Student feedback
- Peer review
- Mentor recommendation

**Certification Levels:**

- **Junior Mentor** — Can review beginner projects
- **Senior Mentor** — Can review intermediate projects
- **Lead Mentor** — Can review advanced projects and mentor other mentors
- **Principal Mentor** — Can design curriculum and lead programs

### Office Hours

**Format:**

- Weekly office hours (2 hours minimum)
- Student booking system
- Individual or group sessions
- Project review sessions
- Career guidance sessions

**Responsibilities:**

- Be available during office hours
- Provide constructive feedback
- Guide student growth
- Document interactions
- Follow up on action items

**Quality Standards:**

- Response time within 24 hours
- Constructive feedback
- Actionable suggestions
- Follow-up on commitments
- Professional communication

### Project Review

**Process:**

- Student submits project for review
- Mentor reviews project
- Mentor provides feedback
- Student responds to feedback
- Mentor verifies improvements
- Project is approved or rejected

**Review Criteria:**

- Code quality
- Documentation quality
- Impact assessment
- Portfolio quality
- Learning evidence
- Open source potential

**Feedback Quality:**

- Constructive and specific
- Actionable suggestions
- Positive reinforcement
- Growth-oriented
- Connected to learning objectives

### Student Feedback

**Format:**

- Regular check-ins (weekly or biweekly)
- Progress review
- Goal setting
- Skill assessment
- Portfolio review
- Career guidance

**Responsibilities:**

- Track student progress
- Identify challenges
- Provide guidance
- Connect to resources
- Celebrate achievements
- Document feedback

**Quality Standards:**

- Regular and consistent
- Specific and actionable
- Positive and encouraging
- Growth-oriented
- Connected to institutional goals

### Research

**Activities:**

- Contribute to Knowledge Packages
- Review curriculum content
- Design assessment criteria
- Develop teaching methods
- Publish findings
- Present at events

**Output:**

- Knowledge Packages
- Curriculum improvements
- Teaching methods
- Research publications
- Open source tools
- Community resources

**Recognition:**

- Attribution in Knowledge Packages
- Publication credits
- Community recognition
- Career advancement

### Promotion

**Levels:**

- **Junior Mentor** → **Senior Mentor**
- **Senior Mentor** → **Lead Mentor**
- **Lead Mentor** → **Principal Mentor**
- **Principal Mentor** → **Program Director**

**Criteria:**

- Student outcomes
- Portfolio quality
- Community impact
- Research contribution
- Leadership evidence
- Peer recognition

**Process:**

- Self-nomination or nomination by peers
- Portfolio review
- Interview with promotion committee
- Community feedback
- Decision and notification

### Recognition

**Types:**

- **Mentor of the Month** — Outstanding monthly contribution
- **Mentor of the Year** — Outstanding annual contribution
- **Teaching Excellence** — Exceptional teaching quality
- **Research Contribution** — Exceptional research contribution
- **Community Leadership** — Exceptional community building
- **Lifetime Achievement** — Career-long contribution

**Process:**

- Community nomination
- Review by recognition committee
- Public announcement
- Portfolio update
- Community celebration

## Responsibilities

### Time Commitment

- Minimum 2 hours per week
- Office hours: 2 hours per week
- Project reviews: 2-3 per week
- Student feedback: As needed
- Research: As available

### Communication

- Respond within 24 hours
- Provide constructive feedback
- Document interactions
- Follow up on commitments
- Maintain professional boundaries

### Quality

- High standards for feedback
- Constructive and specific
- Growth-oriented
- Connected to learning objectives
- Evidence-based

## Support

### From Institution

- Training and certification
- Tools and resources
- Community support
- Recognition and rewards
- Career development

### From Community

- Peer mentoring
- Knowledge sharing
- Collaborative projects
- Research opportunities
- Professional networking

### Mentor Activity

### Community Impact
