# Instructor Lifecycle

## Overview

The Instructor Lifecycle defines how instructors teach and evolve at Bhavya Foundation. Instructors are responsible for course creation, lesson design, curriculum governance, and research integration.

## The Lifecycle

```
Course Creation
  ↓ [Design course structure and objectives]
Lesson Review
  ↓ [Review and improve lesson content]
Curriculum Updates
  ↓ [Integrate research and feedback]
Research Integration
  ↓ [Connect teaching to research]
Knowledge Package Creation
  ↓ [Document institutional knowledge]
Teaching Analytics
  ↓ [Measure teaching effectiveness]
Continuous Improvement
  → [Evolve teaching based on evidence]
```

## Phases

### Course Creation

**Process:**
1. **Define Objectives** — What will students learn?
2. **Design Structure** — How will the course be organized?
3. **Create Lessons** — What content will be taught?
4. **Design Assessments** — How will learning be verified?
5. **Create Projects** — What will students build?
6. **Define Success Criteria** — How will course success be measured?

**Requirements:**
- Clear learning objectives
- Logical course structure
- Engaging lesson content
- Valid assessments
- Meaningful projects
- Measurable success criteria

**Quality Standards:**
- Research-backed content
- Evidence-based pedagogy
- Accessible design
- Inclusive materials
- Continuous improvement plan

### Lesson Review

**Process:**
1. **Content Review** — Is the content accurate and current?
2. **Pedagogy Review** — Is the teaching effective?
3. **Accessibility Review** — Is the content accessible?
4. **Engagement Review** — Is the content engaging?
5. **Assessment Review** — Do assessments measure learning?
6. **Feedback Integration** — Has feedback been addressed?

**Reviewers:**
- Peer instructors
- Mentors
- Students
- AI review
- Community review

**Criteria:**
- Accuracy of content
- Clarity of explanation
- Engagement quality
- Assessment validity
- Accessibility compliance
- Feedback responsiveness

### Curriculum Updates

**Process:**
1. **Gather Evidence** — Collect feedback and data
2. **Analyze Patterns** — Identify improvement opportunities
3. **Design Changes** — Create curriculum improvements
4. **Review Changes** — Get peer and community feedback
5. **Implement Changes** — Update curriculum
6. **Measure Impact** — Assess improvement effectiveness

**Sources of Evidence:**
- Student feedback
- Mentor feedback
- Teaching analytics
- Research findings
- Industry trends
- Community suggestions

**Update Cadence:**
- **Continuous** — Minor fixes and improvements
- **Quarterly** — Significant content updates
- **Annually** — Major curriculum revisions

### Research Integration

**Process:**
1. **Identify Research** — Find relevant research
2. **Evaluate Research** — Assess research quality and relevance
3. **Integrate Research** — Connect research to teaching
4. **Document Integration** — Record research connections
5. **Share Findings** — Publish research insights
6. **Evolve Teaching** — Update teaching based on research

**Research Sources:**
- Academic papers
- Industry publications
- Open source projects
- Community contributions
- Student projects
- Mentor insights

**Integration Methods:**
- Knowledge Packages
- Lesson content
- Project design
- Assessment criteria
- Teaching methods

### Knowledge Package Creation

**Process:**
1. **Identify Knowledge** — What institutional knowledge should be documented?
2. **Research Context** — What research supports this knowledge?
3. **Create Package** — Document the knowledge
4. **Review Package** — Get peer review
5. **Publish Package** — Make available to community
6. **Evolve Package** — Update based on feedback

**Package Contents:**
- Problem statement
- Research background
- Core concepts
- Teaching methods
- Assessment criteria
- Project ideas
- Resources
- References

**Quality Standards:**
- Research-backed
- Peer-reviewed
- Community-validated
- Continuously updated
- Accessible

### Teaching Analytics

**Metrics:**
- Student engagement
- Student satisfaction
- Student outcomes
- Content effectiveness
- Assessment validity
- Community impact

**Data Sources:**
- Student feedback
- Assessment results
- Project quality
- Community engagement
- Mentor feedback
- Peer review

**Analysis:**
- Identify patterns
- Measure effectiveness
- Compare approaches
- Predict outcomes
- Recommend improvements

### Continuous Improvement

**Process:**
1. **Collect Data** — Gather teaching analytics
2. **Analyze Patterns** — Identify improvement opportunities
3. **Design Experiments** — Test new approaches
4. **Measure Results** — Assess experiment outcomes
5. **Implement Best Practices** — Scale successful approaches
6. **Share Learnings** — Document and publish insights

**Improvement Areas:**
- Content quality
- Teaching methods
- Assessment design
- Student engagement
- Learning outcomes
- Community impact
