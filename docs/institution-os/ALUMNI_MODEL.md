# Alumni Model

## Overview

The Alumni Model defines how relationships endure at Bhavya Foundation. Alumni are not former students — they are lifelong community members who continue to contribute, mentor, and grow.

## Alumni Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Lifelong Learning** — Alumni continue to learn and grow
2. **Lifelong Contribution** — Alumni continue to contribute
3. **Lifelong Connection** — Alumni maintain relationships
4. **Lifelong Impact** — Alumni continue to create value
5. **Lifelong Community** — Alumni remain part of the community

### The Alumni Flywheel

```
Learn → Build → Graduate → Mentor → Donate → Hire → Learn
```

Every alumni interaction strengthens the institution.

## Alumni Lifecycle

### 1. Transition to Alumni

**When:** Student completes active role
**Process:**

- Portfolio completion
- Contribution summary
- Reflection essay
- Community farewell
- Alumni registration

**Requirements:**

- Active role completion
- Portfolio with contributions
- Community standing
- Professional development

### 2. Alumni Engagement

**Activities:**

- Community participation
- Mentoring students
- Guest lectures
- Research collaboration
- Event attendance
- Donation
- Hiring

**Frequency:**

- Regular community engagement
- Monthly mentoring
- Quarterly guest lectures
- Annual events
- Ongoing donations

### 3. Alumni Leadership

**Roles:**

- **Alumni Mentor** — Guide student growth
- **Alumni Instructor** — Teach courses
- **Alumni Researcher** — Collaborate on research
- **Alumni Organizer** — Organize events
- **Alumni Ambassador** — Represent institution
- **Alumni Advisor** — Advise institution

**Requirements:**

- Active alumni engagement
- Leadership evidence
- Community recognition
- Institutional contribution

### 4. Alumni Legacy

**Activities:**

- Long-term mentoring
- Major donations
- Partnership development
- Policy influence
- Institutional governance
- Legacy projects

**Requirements:**

- Extended alumni engagement
- Significant contribution
- Community recognition
- Institutional impact
