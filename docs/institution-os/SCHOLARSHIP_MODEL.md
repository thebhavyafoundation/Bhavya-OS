# Scholarship Model

## Overview

The Scholarship Model defines how financial barriers are removed at Bhavya Foundation. Scholarships are not charity — they are investments in potential.

## Scholarship Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Potential Over Privilege** — Talent is evenly distributed, opportunity is not
2. **Access Over Affordability** — Cost should not prevent learning
3. **Merit Over Means** — Scholarships reward potential, not just need
4. **Impact Over Amount** — Small scholarships can create large impact
5. **Sustainability Over Generosity** — Scholarships should be sustainable

### The Scholarship Flywheel

```
Apply → Review → Award → Support → Impact → Donate → Apply
```

Every scholarship creates future donors and mentors.

## Scholarship Types

### Merit-Based Scholarships

**For:** Students with exceptional potential
**Criteria:**

- Outstanding portfolio
- Strong community contribution
- Exceptional learning outcomes
- Leadership potential
- Innovation potential

**Amount:** Full or partial tuition
**Duration:** Program length

### Need-Based Scholarships

**For:** Students with financial barriers
**Criteria:**

- Financial need
- Learning commitment
- Community potential
- Growth mindset
- Resilience

**Amount:** Partial to full tuition
**Duration:** Program length

### Diversity Scholarships

**For:** Underrepresented groups in AI
**Criteria:**

- Belonging to underrepresented group
- Commitment to diversity
- Community leadership
- Role model potential
- Impact potential

**Amount:** Full or partial tuition
**Duration:** Program length

### Impact Scholarships

**For:** Students solving social impact problems
**Criteria:**

- Social impact project
- Community benefit
- Innovation potential
- Sustainability potential
- Collaboration potential

**Amount:** Full or partial tuition
**Duration:** Program length

### Community Scholarships

**For:** Active community contributors
**Criteria:**

- Community contribution
- Mentoring impact
- Teaching impact
- Research impact
- Leadership impact

**Amount:** Partial tuition
**Duration:** Program length

## Scholarship Lifecycle

### 1. Application

**Requirements:**

- Application form
- Portfolio submission
- Essay submission
- Recommendation letters
- Financial documentation (if need-based)

**Process:**

- Online application
- Document upload
- Essay submission
- Recommendation request
- Application fee waiver (if needed)

### 2. Review

**Process:**

- Application review
- Portfolio assessment
- Essay evaluation
- Recommendation verification
- Financial assessment (if need-based)

**Reviewers:**

- Scholarship committee
- Mentors
- Alumni
- Community members
- AI review

### 3. Interview

**Process:**

- Initial screening
- Detailed interview
- Portfolio presentation
- Community reference check
- Final selection

**Interviewers:**

- Scholarship committee
- Mentors
- Alumni
- Community leaders

### 4. Award

**Process:**

- Award notification
- Acceptance confirmation
- Payment processing
- Welcome package
- Support assignment

**Support:**

- Assigned mentor
- Peer group
- Community access
- Career services
- Alumni network

### 5. Support

**Process:**

- Regular check-ins
- Academic support
- Career guidance
- Community integration
- Challenge resolution

**Support Providers:**

- Assigned mentor
- Peer group
- Community
- Career services
- Alumni network

### 6. Impact
