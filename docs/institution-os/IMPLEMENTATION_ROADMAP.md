- Open source value (not just project completion)

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks

- **Risk:** Runtime integration complexity
- **Mitigation:** Incremental integration, thorough testing

### Community Risks

- **Risk:** Community growth too fast
- **Mitigation:** Scalable processes, community leadership development

### Quality Risks

- **Risk:** Quality dilution with growth
- **Mitigation:** Strong governance, quality assurance, continuous improvement

### Sustainability Risks

- **Risk:** Funding and resource constraints
- **Mitigation:** Diverse revenue streams, volunteer programs, partnerships
