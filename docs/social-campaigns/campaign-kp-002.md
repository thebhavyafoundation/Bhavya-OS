> You open your computer every day. You click icons, switch between windows, and search for files. But are you doing it efficiently?
>
> The average knowledge worker wastes 4 hours per week on navigation tasks — searching for windows, lost files, and unfamiliar menus. That's 200 hours per year spent fighting your computer instead of working with it.
>
> **The 5 skills that change everything:**
>
> 1. **Window snapping** — Drag a window to the edge of your screen to tile it automatically. On Windows, Win+Arrow keys. On macOS, hold the green button.
>
> 2. **Keyboard shortcuts for app switching** — Alt+Tab (Windows) or Cmd+Tab (macOS) cycles through open apps faster than finding them on the taskbar.
>
> 3. **Quick settings access** — Windows: Win+I. macOS: Cmd+, in any app. Don't click through menus.
>
> 4. **System search** — Windows: Win+S. macOS: Cmd+Space. Find files, apps, and settings without navigating folders.
>
> 5. **Task Manager** — Ctrl+Shift+Esc (Windows) or Cmd+Space "Activity Monitor" (macOS). Know what's running on your machine.
>
