# Risks

Identified risks and mitigation strategies.

---

## Technical Risks

### 1. Content OS Complexity

**Risk:** Domain model is too complex to implement in timeline.
**Impact:** High — delays entire project.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Start with MVP subset of entities
- Iterate based on actual needs
- Keep schema flexible

### 2. AI Integration Challenges

**Risk:** AI mentor doesn't provide quality feedback.
**Impact:** Medium — affects learning quality.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Extensive prompt engineering
- A/B test different approaches
- Human fallback always available

### 3. Lab Environment Provisioning

**Risk:** Difficulty creating consistent lab environments.
**Impact:** Medium — affects hands-on learning.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Use containerization (Docker)
- Pre-built environments
- Cloud-based IDEs as fallback

### 4. Scalability

**Risk:** System doesn't scale to many students.
**Impact:** Low — unlikely in early stages.
**Likelihood:** Low.
**Mitigation:**

- Design for scale from start
- Use serverless where possible
- Monitor and optimize

---

## Educational Risks

### 5. Curriculum Quality

**Risk:** Content doesn't effectively teach.
**Impact:** High — core mission failure.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Follow learning philosophy
- Test with beta students
- Iterate based on feedback
- Expert review

### 6. Low Engagement

**Risk:** Students don't complete courses.
**Impact:** High — mission failure.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Gamification
- Community features
- Mentor support
- Regular check-ins

### 7. Assessment Validity

**Risk:** Assessments don't measure real competency.
**Impact:** High — certification meaningless.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Multiple assessment types
- Portfolio-based evidence
- Mentor validation
- Real-world projects

## Operational Risks

### 8. Mentor Availability

**Risk:** Not enough mentors for students.
**Impact:** Medium — affects quality.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- AI mentor handles routine questions
- Mentor training program
- Community peer support
- Scalable office hours

### 9. Content Maintenance

**Risk:** Content becomes outdated.
**Impact:** Medium — affects quality.
**Likelihood:** High.
**Mitigation:**

- Version control
- Regular review cycle
- Community contributions
- Automated checks

### 10. Cost Overrun

**Risk:** AI usage costs exceed budget.
**Impact:** Medium — affects sustainability.
**Likelihood:** Medium.
**Mitigation:**

- Usage monitoring
- Cost optimization
- Tiered AI access
- Sponsorship model

## Market Risks

### 11. Competition

**Risk:** Other platforms offer similar value.
**Impact:** Medium — affects enrollment.
**Likelihood:** High.
**Mitigation:**

- Unique value proposition
- Open source advantage
- Community moat
- Quality differentiation

### 12. Low Demand

**Risk:** Not enough students want to learn AI.
**Impact:** High — mission failure.
**Likelihood:** Low.
**Mitigation:**

- Market research
- Free tier
- Partnerships
- Content marketing
