# Bhavya AI Curriculum Constitution

**Document:** 11 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-08-07 | **Status:** Constitutional

---

## Preamble

This document establishes the academic framework of the Bhavya AI Institute. It defines the schools, programs, courses, competencies, and learning outcomes that constitute the institution's educational mission.

The curriculum is designed to produce graduates who are not merely knowledgeable, but capable. Every student graduates with evidence of ability—projects, research, open-source contributions—not certificates alone.

---

## Article 1: Academic Structure

### 1.1 Institutional Hierarchy

```
Bhavya AI Institute
│
├── Schools (12)
│   ├── School of AI Foundations
│   ├── School of Machine Learning
│   ├── School of Deep Learning
│   ├── School of LLM Engineering
│   ├── School of AI Agents
│   ├── School of Robotics
│   ├── School of Data Engineering
│   ├── School of Mathematics
│   ├── School of Research
│   ├── School of AI Systems
│   ├── School of AI Safety
│   └── School of Open Source Engineering
│
├── Research Centers (3)
│   ├── AI Research Center
│   ├── Open Source Lab
│   └── Innovation Lab
│
├── Publications (2)
│   ├── Bhavya AI Journal
│   └── Technical Reports
│
└── Community
    ├── Student Community
    ├── Alumni Network
    └── Industry Partners
```

### 1.2 School Independence

Each school operates with academic autonomy while adhering to constitutional principles. New schools may be added without disrupting existing structure.

### 1.3 Cross-School Integration

Students may enroll in courses across schools. Prerequisites are enforced. Competencies are transferable.
