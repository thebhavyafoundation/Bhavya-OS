## Article 2: Governance Bodies

### 2.1 Board of Directors

**Authority:** Supreme governing body

**Responsibilities:**

1. Strategic direction
2. Financial oversight
3. Constitutional amendments

**Responsibilities:**

1. Course design

3. Assessment design
4. Industry alignment

### 2.4 Research Committee

**Authority:** Research standards

**Responsibilities:**

1. Research ethics

3. Reproducibility

5. Collaboration

### 2.5 Ethics Board

**Authority:** AI ethics
**Composition:** Ethicists, legal experts, public representatives
**Responsibilities:**

1. AI ethics review

3. Privacy protection
4. Safety standards

1. Student feedback

## Article 9: Amendment Process

### 9.1 Amendment Proposal

Amendments may be proposed by:

1. Board of Directors
2. Academic Council
3. Curriculum Board
4. Research Committee
5. Ethics Board
6. Student Government
7. Faculty (10% petition)
8. Students (20% petition)

### 9.2 Amendment Process

1. **Proposal** — Written proposal with rationale
2. **Review** — Relevant committee review
3. **Public comment** — 30-day comment period
4. **Revision** — Incorporate feedback
5. **Vote** — Appropriate authority vote
6. **Ratification** — Board ratification (for constitutional amendments)
7. **Implementation** — Phased rollout
8. **Communication** — Announce to all stakeholders

### 9.3 Emergency Amendments

In case of urgent need:

1. **Emergency committee** — Convened within 24 hours
2. **Emergency vote** — 2/3 of committee
3. **Temporary implementation** — 90-day limit
4. **Full process** — Must follow normal process within 90 days
