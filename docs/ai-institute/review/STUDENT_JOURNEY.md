# Student Journey Review — AI Institute v1.0

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Perspective:** Complete beginner with no AI experience

## Journey Walkthrough

### Step 1: Landing Page

**Was the next action obvious?**
Yes. "Take the Assessment" is clear and prominent.

**Was I motivated to continue?**
Moderately. The claims ("100+ Students", "50+ Projects Built") feel inflated. The mission statement is compelling but the stats undermine trust.

**What confused me?**
The difference between "AI Institute" and "Bhavya Foundation" is unclear. Which is the product?

**What felt unnecessary?**
The "How It Works" section duplicates the features section.

**Did I actually learn something?**
No. I learned what the product claims to be, not what it does.

**Would I return tomorrow?**
Maybe. The mission is compelling enough to try the assessment.

---

### Step 2: AI Readiness Assessment

**Was the next action obvious?**
Yes. "Start Assessment" button is clear.

**Was I motivated to continue?**
Yes. The promise of a personalized roadmap is appealing.

**What confused me?**
The assessment mixes knowledge questions (programming, math) with preference questions (goals, time). The scoring doesn't explain how preferences affect the roadmap.

**What felt unnecessary?**
The "What we're measuring" section is vague. Why does programming knowledge matter for an AI course?

**Did I actually learn something?**
No. I learned what the product thinks I should know, not what I'll learn.

**Would I return tomorrow?**
Yes, if the roadmap is genuinely personalized.
