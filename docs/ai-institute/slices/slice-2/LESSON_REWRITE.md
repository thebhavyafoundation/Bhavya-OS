# Lesson Rewrite — From Content to Interaction

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Scope:** Rewrite Lesson 1 (Introduction to AI) for interaction-first learning

---

## Current Lesson 1 Structure

```
Lesson 1: Introduction to AI (20 minutes)
├── Reading (10 minutes) — Passive consumption
├── Examples (3 minutes) — Passive consumption
├── Exercises (5 minutes) — Instructions only, no AI
├── Reflect (1 minute) — Text area, no feedback
└── Notes (1 minute) — Text area, local storage
```

**Problem:** 70% passive consumption, 30% active (but broken)

---

## Rewritten Lesson 1 Structure

```
Lesson 1: Introduction to AI (30 minutes)
├── Objective (1 minute) — What you'll build
├── Quick Read (3 minutes) — Core concept
├── AI Playground (10 minutes) — Experiment with AI
├── Reflection (3 minutes) — What did you learn?
├── Iteration (5 minutes) — Improve your prompt
├── Mini Project (5 minutes) — Build something real
├── AI Review (2 minutes) — Get feedback
└── Portfolio Export (1 minute) — Save your work
```

**Target:** 30% explanation, 70% interaction

---

## Detailed Lesson Flow

### Step 1: Objective (1 minute)

**What you'll learn:**

- What AI is in your own words
- How to talk to AI effectively

**What you'll build:**

- A prompt that explains AI to anyone
- Your first portfolio artifact

**What you'll leave with:**

- Evidence that you understand AI
- A reusable prompt template

---

### Step 2: Quick Read (3 minutes)

**Core concept:**

> AI is software that learns from data instead of being explicitly programmed. When you write a prompt, you're activating patterns the AI learned during training.

**Key terms:**

- **AI** — Software that learns from data
- **Prompt** — Instructions you give to AI
- **Pattern** — What AI learned from training data

**That's it.** No 10-minute reading. No overwhelming content. Just the core concept.

---

### Step 3: AI Playground (10 minutes)

**Your first experiment:**

1. Open the AI Playground
2. Write a prompt asking AI to explain what AI is
3. See the response
4. Notice: Is the response good? Bad? What's missing?

**Experiment 1:**

```
Your prompt: [Write anything about AI]
AI response: [See what happens]
```

**Reflection prompt:**

- What did the AI say?
- Was it helpful? Why or why not?
- What's missing from the response?

**Experiment 2:**
Now improve your prompt. Add:

- Who is the audience?
- What format do you want?
- What should the AI avoid?

```
Your prompt: [Improved prompt]
AI response: [See the difference]
```

**Reflection prompt:**

- What changed in your prompt?
- How did the output improve?
- What did you learn about prompting?

---

### Step 4: Reflection (3 minutes)

**Structured reflection:**

1. **What changed between your first and second prompt?**
   - [Text area]

2. **Why did those changes improve the output?**
   - [Text area]

3. **What surprised you about how AI responds to different prompts?**
   - [Text area]

**AI Mentor feedback:**
After submitting reflection, the AI mentor responds:

> "You discovered that specificity matters. Your first prompt was vague, your second gave the AI clear constraints. This is the foundation of prompt engineering."

---

### Step 5: Iteration (5 minutes)

**Challenge:**

Now try to make your prompt even better. Add:

- An example of the output you want
- A constraint the AI should follow
- A format for the response

```
Your prompt: [Even better prompt]
AI response: [Best response yet]
```

**Compare your three attempts:**

| Attempt | Prompt                                        | Score  | Improvement |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------- |
| #1      | "What is AI?"                                 | 30/100 | —           |
| #2      | "Explain AI to a beginner"                    | 60/100 | +100%       |
| #3      | "Explain AI to a 12-year-old using analogies" | 85/100 | +142%       |

**What you learned:**

- Specificity improves output
- Audience matters
- Format constraints help
- Iteration is essential

---

### Step 6: Mini Project (5 minutes)

**Build something real:**

Using what you learned, create a prompt that:

1. Explains AI to a specific audience
2. Uses a specific format
3. Includes at least one example
4. Has clear constraints

**This is your portfolio artifact.**

---

### Step 7: AI Review (2 minutes)

**Get feedback on your prompt:**

The AI mentor reviews your prompt and provides:

- Strengths
- Improvements
- Score (0-100)

**Example feedback:**

> "Strong prompt! You specified the audience (12-year-old), format (step-by-step), and included an analogy. Score: 85/100. To improve: add what the AI should avoid."

---

### Step 8: Portfolio Export (1 minute)

**Save your work:**

Export your portfolio artifact:

- Title: "My First AI Prompt"
- Skills: Prompt structuring, audience awareness, iteration
- Evidence: All 3 attempts with reflections
- Score: 85/100

**You now have:**

1. A prompt that works
2. Evidence of improvement
3. A portfolio artifact
4. Skills you can demonstrate
