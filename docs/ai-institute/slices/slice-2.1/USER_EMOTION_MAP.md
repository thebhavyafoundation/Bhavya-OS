# User Emotion Map

## Emotional Journey

### Screen 1: Welcome

**Emotion:** Curiosity + Mild Anxiety
**What They Feel:**

- "What is this?"
- "Will this be hard?"
- "What do I do?"

**What They Need:**

- Clear, simple instructions
- Reassurance
- Quick start

### Screen 2: First Prompt

**Emotion:** Excitement + Nervousness
**What They Feel:**

- "I get to try something!"
- "What if I mess up?"
- "This is happening fast"

**What They Need:**

- Zero barriers
- Immediate feedback
- Safe space to fail

### Screen 3: Experiment

**Emotion:** Surprise + Curiosity
**What They Feel:**

- "That's interesting!"
- "I didn't expect that"
- "What if I try something else?"

**What They Need:**

- Clear feedback
- Options to retry
- No judgment

### Screen 4: Reflect

**Emotion:** Thoughtfulness + Pride
**What They Feel:**

- "I learned something"
- "I can do this"
- "I want to try more"

**What They Need:**

- Time to think
- Meaningful questions
- Validation

### Screen 5: Build

**Emotion:** Confidence + Motivation
**What They Feel:**

- "I know what to do now"
- "I can make this better"
- "I want to see if it works"

**What They Need:**

- Clear checklist
- Progress feedback
- Celebration

### Screen 6: Portfolio

**Emotion:** Pride + Accomplishment
**What They Feel:**

- "I made this"
- "I can show this to someone"
- "I want to do the next lesson"

**What They Need:**

- Tangible artifact
- Shareable proof
- Next step

## Design Principles for Emotions

1. **Reduce anxiety** — No walls of text, no complex navigation
2. **Build confidence** — Celebrate small wins, show progress
3. **Encourage curiosity** — Ask questions, don't give answers
4. **Create pride** — Generate artifacts, show evidence
5. **Maintain momentum** — Clear next steps, no dead ends
