# First 60 Seconds

## The Critical Window

The first 60 seconds determine whether a student continues or abandons the lesson.

## Timeline

### 0-10 seconds: Landing

- Student sees welcome screen
- AI mentor introduces itself
- No reading — just a friendly greeting
- Clear "Start" button

### 10-20 seconds: Context

- What will happen (3 bullet points)
- What they'll build (1 sentence)
- Estimated time (visible but not prominent)

### 20-30 seconds: Transition

- Click "Start First Experiment"
- Screen changes to interactive playground
- No intermediate pages

### 30-60 seconds: First Interaction

- Student writes their first prompt
- AI responds
- Student sees results
- They've done something real

## Design Rules

### Must Have

- Zero prerequisites
- No reading before interaction
- Immediate feedback
- Clear progress indicator
- Safe failure environment

### Must Not Have

- Walls of text
- Complex navigation
- Multiple choices
- Confusing instructions
- Dead ends

## Success Metrics

- Student writes first prompt within 60 seconds
- Student sees AI response within 10 seconds of writing
- Student can retry immediately
- Student feels "I did something" not "I read something"

## Anti-Patterns

- "Read this first"
- "Before you begin"
- "Important instructions"
- "Make sure you understand"
- "This is required reading"
