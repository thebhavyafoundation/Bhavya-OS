# Portfolio Example

## Artifact: AI Explanation Prompt v1

### Title

AI Explanation Prompt v1

### Summary

A prompt template designed to explain artificial intelligence to a 12-year-old, refined through experimentation and reflection.

### Skills Demonstrated

- Prompt Engineering
- Iteration
- Reflection
- Communication
- Critical Thinking

### Evidence

**First Attempt:**
"Explain AI to a 12-year-old"

**Improved Version:**
"You are a friendly teacher explaining to a 12-year-old who loves video games. Explain artificial intelligence using examples from games they play. Keep it under 100 words and make it exciting."

### Reflection

**What surprised you?**
The first response was too technical. I didn't realize how much the wording matters.

**What would you do differently?**
I would think about who I'm talking to before writing the prompt.

**What did you learn?**
Specificity matters. The more context you give, the better the response.
