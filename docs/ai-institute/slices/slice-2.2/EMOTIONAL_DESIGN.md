# Emotional Design

## The Emotional Journey

### Screen 1: Welcome

**Emotion:** Curiosity
**What They Feel:** "This looks interesting. What will happen?"
**Design:** Friendly mentor, clear steps, no jargon

### Screen 2: Experiment — First Attempt

**Emotion:** Excitement + Mild Anxiety
**What They Feel:** "I get to try something! What do I type?"
**Design:** Simple text box, clear instruction, no judgment

### Screen 3: Experiment — AI Response

**Emotion:** Surprise
**What They Feel:** "The AI responded! What did it say?"
**Design:** Real AI response (not a score), mentor observation

### Screen 4: Experiment — Mentor Coaching

**Emotion:** Curiosity
**What They Feel:** "What should I try next?"
**Design:** Mentor suggests, doesn't tell. Questions, not answers.

### Screen 5: Experiment — Improved Attempt

**Emotion:** Determination
**What They Feel:** "Let me try something different"
**Design:** Clear guidance, no pressure

### Screen 6: Experiment — Better Response

**Emotion:** Surprise + Delight
**What They Feel:** "The AI responded better! My improvement worked!"
**Design:** Better AI response, celebration message

### Screen 7: Comparison View

**Emotion:** Pride
**What They Feel:** "Look at that difference! I did that!"
**Design:** Side-by-side comparison, highlight changes

### Screen 8: Build

**Emotion:** Confidence
**What They Feel:** "I know what to do now"
**Design:** Checklist, clear targets

### Screen 9: Portfolio

**Emotion:** Accomplishment
**What They Feel:** "I have something to show for this"
**Design:** Shareable artifact, reusable pattern

### Screen 10: Done

**Emotion:** Motivation
**What They Feel:** "I want to try more"
**Design:** Next step clear, achievement recognized

## Critical Emotions

### 1. Surprise (Screen 3 & 6)

The student must be surprised by the AI's response. This creates curiosity and engagement.

### 2. Pride (Screen 7)

The student must feel proud of their improvement. This reinforces learning.

### 3. Accomplishment (Screen 9)

The student must feel like they built something. This creates motivation.

## What Would Break the Emotions

1. **Showing scores instead of responses** — No surprise, no delight
2. **No comparison view** — No pride, no accomplishment
3. **No mentor coaching** — No guidance, no encouragement
4. **No celebration** — No recognition of progress
5. **Weak portfolio** — No tangible evidence of learning
