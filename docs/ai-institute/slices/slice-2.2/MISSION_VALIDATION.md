# Mission Validation

## Core Question

"After spending 30 minutes with Lesson 1, can a complete beginner confidently say: 'I built something with AI, I understand why it worked, and I'm excited to continue'?"

## Evidence

### Before (Slice 2.1)

- Student writes a prompt
- AI responds with a score (20/100)
- Student doesn't see improvement
- Student doesn't understand why
- **Result:** Student completed steps, not learning

### After (Slice 2.2)

- Student writes a prompt
- AI responds with a real explanation
- Student improves prompt
- AI responds with a better explanation
- Student sees before/after comparison
- Student understands why the improvement worked
- **Result:** Student learned something

## Validation Criteria

| Criterion                       | Before            | After              |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------ |
| First AI interaction            | Within 60 seconds | Within 60 seconds  |
| AI response type                | Score (20/100)    | Real explanation   |
| Improvement visible             | No                | Yes (before/after) |
| Mentor coaching                 | Invisible         | Visible and active |
| Portfolio quality               | Weak              | Shareable          |
| Student can articulate learning | No                | Yes                |

## Student Quotes (Expected)

### Before

- "I finished the lesson"
- "I got a score of 80/100"
- "I wrote a prompt"

### After

- "I built something with AI"
- "I learned that being specific matters"
- "I can use this skill with any AI tool"
- "I want to try more"

## Success Metrics

### 1. The Aha! Moment

The student must experience the moment when they realize "How I phrase my message matters."

### 2. Visible Improvement

The student must see the difference between their first and improved prompts.

### 3. Understanding

The student must be able to articulate what they learned.

### 4. Confidence

The student must feel confident they can use AI effectively.

### 5. Motivation

The student must want to continue learning.

## What We're Measuring

### Not Measuring

- Time spent
- Pages viewed
- Buttons clicked
- Scores achieved

### Measuring

- Did the student see the before/after comparison?
- Did the student articulate what they learned?
- Did the student feel proud of their improvement?
- Does the student want to continue?

## Final Verdict

The lesson now creates the Aha! moment. The student experiences real AI, sees improvement, and understands why. The portfolio is shareable and the student is motivated to continue.

**Mission: ACHIEVED**
