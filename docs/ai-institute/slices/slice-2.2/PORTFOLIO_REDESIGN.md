# Portfolio Redesign

## From Weak to Shareable

### Before (Slice 2.1)

```
Title: AI Explanation Prompt v1
Summary: A prompt template designed to explain AI to a 12-year-old
Skills: Prompt Engineering, Iteration, Reflection
Your Prompt: [text]
```

### After (Slice 2.2)

```
Title: AI Explanation Prompt v1
Problem: Explain AI to a 12-year-old
Best Prompt: [text]
Skills: Prompt Engineering, Iteration, Reflection, AI Communication
What I Learned: [paragraph]
Reusable Pattern: Explain [TOPIC] to [AUDIENCE] using [FORMAT]
Export: Copy | Download | Share on LinkedIn
```

## What's New

### 1. Problem Statement

The portfolio now includes what the student was trying to solve, not just what they built.

### 2. What I Learned

The student articulates what they learned, not just what they did.

### 3. Reusable Prompt Pattern

The student gets a template they can use again, not just a one-time prompt.

### 4. Share on LinkedIn

The student can share their achievement with a click.

## Why This Works

### 1. Shareable

The student has something they're proud to show others.

### 2. Reusable

The student gets a skill they can apply elsewhere.

### 3. Evidence of Learning

The portfolio proves the student learned something, not just completed steps.

### 4. Career-Relevant

The portfolio demonstrates skills that matter in the workplace.

## Share on LinkedIn Template

```
Just completed my first AI experiment at Bhavya AI Institute!
```
