# Problem Library

## Overview

The Problem Library contains real-world problems that students can choose to solve. Every problem is backed by evidence and has clear stakeholders, constraints, and success criteria.

## Categories

### Education

- Rural Student Access to AI Tutoring

### Agriculture

- Small Farmer Crop Disease Detection

### Healthcare

- Elderly Companion Chatbot

### NGOs

- NGO Impact Measurement Dashboard

### Local Government

- Local Government Service Chatbot

### Environment

- Disaster Response Coordination

### Small Business

- Small Business Inventory Optimizer

### Accessibility

- Accessible Document Reader

## Problem Structure

Every problem contains:

- Problem statement
- Stakeholders
- Background
- Constraints
- Success criteria
- Knowledge packages
- Related repositories
- Suggested AI techniques
- Difficulty
- Estimated duration
- Open-source opportunity

## Why Real Problems

### Traditional Approach

Students build toy projects that demonstrate skills but don't create impact.

### Bhavya Approach

Students solve real problems that affect real people. The project creates value beyond the student's learning.

## Selection Criteria

Problems are selected based on:

1. Real-world relevance
2. Clear stakeholders
3. Measurable impact
4. Open-source potential
5. AI applicability
6. Student accessibility
