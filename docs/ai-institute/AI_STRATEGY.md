## AI Ethics in Education

### Principles

1. AI assists, doesn't replace learning
2. Students must understand what AI does
3. AI feedback is a starting point, not final answer
4. Human mentor overrides AI
5. Transparency in AI evaluation

### Boundaries

- AI doesn't complete student work
- AI doesn't replace human judgment on certification
- AI doesn't make career decisions
- AI doesn't share student data without consent
- AI is always optional (can request human review)
