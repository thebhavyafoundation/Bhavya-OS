# Product Identity

## Does Bhavya AI Institute feel like...

### ❌ LMS

**No.** There are no modules, no quizzes, no grades, no certificates. The flow is linear and interactive, not grid-based and completion-driven.

### ❌ Course platform

**Partially.** The structure (lesson → lab → check → project) is course-like. But the interaction model is different.

### ❌ Tutorial site

**No.** There are no step-by-step instructions with screenshots. The student does, not follows.

### ✅ AI Workshop

**Almost.** The student is doing something with AI. But the workshop feel is weakened by:

- The mock AI responses (not real AI)
- The lack of real-time coaching
- The static portfolio artifact

### ✅ AI Laboratory

**Almost.** The experiment → reflect → build flow is lab-like. But:

- The experiments are not real (mock provider)
- The results are not visible (no AI response to improved prompt)
- The evidence is weak (no before/after comparison)

### ✅ AI Studio

**No.** A studio implies creative production. The student is not creating anything lasting.

---

## Verdict: AI Workshop (with gaps)

The identity is **AI Workshop** but with significant gaps:

### What Makes It a Workshop

1. The student does, not reads
2. There's experimentation
3. There's reflection
4. There's a tangible output

### What Breaks the Workshop Feel

1. The AI is mock, not real
2. The student doesn't see results of improvement
3. The portfolio is not impressive
4. There's no real coaching

---

## What Would Make It a True AI Workshop

### 1. Real AI Interaction

Even with mock, simulate real responses. Show what the AI WOULD say, not a score.

### 2. Visible Improvement

Show the student the difference between their first and improved prompt.

### 3. Real Coaching

The mentor should ask questions, not give scores.

### 4. Impressive Output

The portfolio should be something the student is proud to share.

### 5. Community

A workshop has other people. Add a "See what others built" section.

---

## Identity Alignment Score: 6/10

The product is trying to be a workshop but is currently a hybrid of workshop and course. The interactive elements are strong, but the mock AI and static portfolio weaken the identity.
