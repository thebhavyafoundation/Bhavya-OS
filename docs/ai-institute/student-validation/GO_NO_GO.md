# GO / NO-GO Decision

## Decision: GO WITH IMPROVEMENTS

---

## Evidence

### What Works

1. **Flow:** Linear, clear, no dead ends
2. **Checklist:** Actionable, confidence-building
3. **Reflection:** Mandatory, thoughtful
4. **Identity:** Workshop, not course
5. **First 60 Seconds:** Student is doing something immediately

### What's Broken

1. **Mock AI:** Student never experiences real AI
2. **No Payoff:** Student doesn't see improvement
3. **Invisible Mentor:** Student interacts with scoring, not coaching
4. **Weak Portfolio:** Not shareable or impressive
5. **Opaque Scoring:** Student doesn't understand 20/100

### What Needs to Change

1. Show AI responses to improved prompts
2. Connect the AIMentor to the playground
3. Replace scoring with coaching
4. Show before/after comparison
5. Make portfolio shareable

---

## Why Not GO

The core learning moment is missing. The student writes an improved prompt but never sees if it works better. Without this payoff, the lesson is a series of steps, not a learning experience.

## Why Not MAJOR REDESIGN

The foundation is solid. The flow, checklist, reflection, and identity are right. The issues are implementation gaps, not design flaws.

---

## Required Changes (Before Release)

### Change 1: Show the Payoff

After the student writes their improved prompt, IMMEDIATELY show the AI's response to it. This is the critical moment.

### Change 2: Connect the Mentor

The AIMentor should coach after each experiment. Display mentor feedback in the playground.

### Change 3: Replace Scoring with Coaching

Instead of "20/100", say "Your prompt is short. Try adding who this is for."

### Change 4: Show Before/After

Display the first attempt and improved attempt side-by-side with their AI responses.

### Change 5: Make Portfolio Shareable

Add a "Share on LinkedIn" button that generates a post with the before/after comparison.

---

## Timeline

These changes can be implemented in 1-2 days. They are implementation fixes, not design changes.

---

## Success Criteria

After implementing these changes, the lesson should achieve:

- **Beginner confidence:** "I built something with AI" (not "I finished a lesson")
- **Portfolio quality:** Something the student would share on LinkedIn
- **Interaction ratio:** 70%+ (currently ~60%)
- **Mentor effectiveness:** 7/10 (currently 2/10)
- **Overall score:** 8/10 (currently 5.5/10)

---

## Final Word

The lesson is 70% there. The remaining 30% is the difference between "I completed a lesson" and "I built something with AI."

**GO WITH IMPROVEMENTS.**
