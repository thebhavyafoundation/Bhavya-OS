# Beginner Walkthrough — Lesson 1

## Persona: Complete Beginner

"I've heard of AI but never used it. I don't code. I want to learn what AI is and how to use it."

---

## Screen 1: Welcome

### Initial Expectation

"I'm going to read about AI and maybe watch a video."

### Actual Experience

I see a welcome screen with a robot emoji and a friendly message: "Hello! I'm here to help you explore AI by doing, not just reading. You'll write your first prompt in about 60 seconds. Ready?"

There are 5 steps listed and a description of what I'll build: "A working prompt template that explains AI to a 12-year-old."

### Confusion

Minimal. The steps are clear. The "Start First Experiment" button is obvious.

### Time Spent

30 seconds.

### Motivation

High. I want to see what happens when I click the button.

### Confidence

Medium. I'm not sure what a "prompt" is, but the button says "Start First Experiment" which sounds like I'll find out.

### Learning Outcome

I know I'm about to do something, not read something.

### Questions Remaining

What is a prompt? What will the AI do?

---

## Screen 2: First Prompt

### Initial Expectation

"I'll see a text box and type something."

### Actual Experience

I see a text area labeled "Your Prompt" with placeholder text "Write your prompt here..." and a "Run Prompt" button.

Above it: "Experiment 1 of 3" and "Write a prompt that asks AI to explain artificial intelligence to a 12-year-old."

I type: "Explain AI to a 12 year old"

I click "Run Prompt."

### Confusion

I don't know what a "prompt" is. The instruction says "Write a prompt" but I don't know what that means. I type anyway because the text box is there.

### Time Spent

45 seconds.

### Motivation

High. I'm doing something.

### Confidence

Medium. I'm typing but not sure if I'm doing it right.

### Learning Outcome

I learned that a prompt is just what you type to talk to AI.

### Questions Remaining

Is that good? What happens now?

---

## Screen 3: AI Response

### Initial Expectation

"Something will happen after I click Run."

### Actual Experience

The AI responds:

"I'd like to help, but I need more information to give you a useful response.

Try adding:

- What specifically you want to know
- Who this is for
- What format you'd like the answer in

Example: 'Explain how AI learns to a beginner using a simple analogy'

Your prompt scored 20/100 for clarity and specificity."

I also see a "Mentor Feedback" box: "Good first attempt. Now look at the output. Is this what you wanted? What is missing? Try adding more detail about who this is for and what format you want."

### Confusion

The AI response is NOT what I expected. I asked it to explain AI and it told me my prompt was bad. This is confusing and slightly discouraging.

The mentor feedback is helpful but I don't understand what "who this is for" means in context.

### Time Spent

1 minute.

### Motivation

Dropped. The AI told me I did it wrong.

### Confidence

Low. I failed my first attempt.

### Learning Outcome

I learned that how you ask matters. But the lesson could have been gentler.

### Questions Remaining

How do I make it better? What does "score" mean? Is 20/100 bad?

---

## Screen 4: Reflection

### Initial Expectation

"I'll write about what I learned."

### Actual Experience

Two text boxes:

1. "What surprised you about the AI's response?"
2. "What would you do differently next time?"

And 4 emoji buttons: Confusing, Okay, Good, Amazing.

I type: "It didn't answer my question. I was surprised. I would try to be more specific."

I click "Good" emoji.

I click "Continue to Build."

### Confusion

The reflection is optional in the playground but required in the lesson flow. I'm not sure if my reflection is saved.

### Time Spent

2 minutes.

### Motivation

Medium. I'm thinking about what happened.

### Confidence

Medium. I understand the pattern now.

### Learning Outcome

I learned that I need to be more specific with AI.

### Questions Remaining

None significant.

---

## Screen 5: Build

### Initial Expectation

"I'll improve my prompt."

### Actual Experience

A text area labeled "Your Improved Prompt" with a checklist:

- At least 50 characters
- Mentions AI or artificial intelligence
- Asks for an explanation
- Reflection completed

I type: "Explain artificial intelligence to a 12 year old who is curious about technology. Use simple words and give 3 real examples they would recognize from everyday life."

The checklist shows all green.

I click "Generate Portfolio Artifact."

### Confusion

The checklist is helpful. I know exactly what to do.

### Time Spent

2 minutes.

### Motivation

High. I'm building something.

### Confidence

High. The checklist gives me confidence.

### Learning Outcome

I learned that being specific produces better results.

### Questions Remaining

Will the AI respond better this time?

---

## Screen 6: Portfolio

### Initial Expectation

"I'll see something I made."

### Actual Experience

"Your First Artifact" with:

- Title: AI Explanation Prompt v1
- Summary: A prompt template designed to explain AI to a 12-year-old
- Skills: Prompt Engineering, Iteration, Reflection
- My improved prompt displayed
- Export options: Copy to Clipboard, Download as JSON

### Confusion

I expected to see the AI's response to my improved prompt. I never got to see if it worked better.

### Time Spent

1 minute.

### Motivation

Medium. I see something I wrote but not the result.

### Confidence

Medium. I have a prompt but no proof it works.

### Learning Outcome

I have a prompt template. But I don't know if it actually produces better output.

### Questions Remaining

Did my improved prompt work? Can I see the AI's response to it?

---

## Screen 7: Done

### Initial Expectation

"I'm done."

### Actual Experience

"Lesson Complete! You just built something with AI. That's real learning."

Achievement: First AI Experiment

Links: Dashboard, Next: Lab

### Confusion

None.

### Time Spent

10 seconds.

### Motivation

Medium. I'm done but not sure what I accomplished.

### Confidence

Medium. I have a prompt but no real proof it works.

### Learning Outcome

I completed the lesson.

### Questions Remaining

What did I actually learn? What can I do with this prompt?

---

## Summary

### Total Time: ~8 minutes

### Key Findings

1. **The AI telling me I failed on first try was discouraging.** A beginner needs gentler feedback.
2. **I never saw the AI respond to my improved prompt.** The lesson ends before showing the payoff.
3. **The portfolio is static.** It shows what I wrote, not what I achieved.
4. **The reflection is valuable.** But it feels disconnected from the experiment.
5. **The checklist is excellent.** Clear, actionable, confidence-building.
6. **The flow is good.** But the emotional arc has a dip at Screen 3 that could lose beginners.
