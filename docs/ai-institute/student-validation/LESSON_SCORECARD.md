## What's Working

### 1. The Flow

The linear screen progression (Welcome → First Prompt → Reflect → Build → Portfolio → Done) is excellent. No tabs, no dead ends, clear progression.

### 2. The Checklist

The build screen checklist is the best part of the lesson. It gives clear, actionable targets and visual progress feedback.

### 3. The First 60 Seconds

The student IS doing something within 60 seconds. The "Start First Experiment" button is clear and the transition to the playground is immediate.

### 4. The Reflection

Mandatory reflection is the right design. The questions are thoughtful and encourage genuine thinking.

### 5. The Identity

The product clearly wants to be a workshop, not a course. The intent is right.

---

## What's Broken

### 1. The Mock AI (Critical)

The MockProvider responds with scores and generic messages, not actual AI responses. The student never experiences real AI.

**Impact:** The student doesn't learn what AI actually does. They learn to game a scoring system.

### 2. No Payoff (Critical)

The student writes an improved prompt but never sees if it works better. The lesson ends before showing the result.

**Impact:** The student doesn't experience the core learning moment: "My improved prompt got a better response."

### 3. The Mentor is Invisible (Critical)

The AIMentor class exists but is never called. The student interacts with a scoring system, not a coach.

**Impact:** The student doesn't feel guided. They feel judged.

### 4. The Portfolio is Weak (Major)

The portfolio shows what the student typed, not what they achieved. It's not shareable or impressive.

**Impact:** The student doesn't have evidence of learning they're proud of.

### 5. The Scoring is Opaque (Major)

The student sees "20/100" but doesn't understand what the numbers mean or how to improve.

**Impact:** The student feels judged without understanding why.

---

## What Needs to Change

### Must Fix Before GO

1. **Show AI responses to improved prompts** — The student must see the payoff
2. **Connect the AIMentor** — The mentor must actually coach
3. **Replace scoring with coaching** — "Your prompt is short" instead of "20/100"
4. **Show before/after comparison** — The student must see their improvement

### Should Fix Before GO

5. **Make the portfolio shareable** — Add a "Share on LinkedIn" feature
6. **Remove jargon** — Don't use "prompt" or "artifact" in the first interaction
7. **Soften the first AI response** — Don't tell the student they failed on first try

### Nice to Have

8. **Add transfer task** — Can the student apply the skill to a new situation?
9. **Add community** — Show what others built
10. **Add celebration** — More emphasis on progress and achievement

---

## Recommendation: GO WITH IMPROVEMENTS

The foundation is solid. The flow, checklist, reflection, and identity are right. But the core learning moment (seeing improvement) is missing, and the mentor is not functioning.

With 4 critical fixes, this lesson could score 8/10.
