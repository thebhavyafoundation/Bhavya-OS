## Critical Drops

### Drop 1: Screen 3 (AI Response)

**Emotion:** Excitement → Disappointment
**Why:** The AI told the student their prompt was bad (20/100) instead of helping them improve.
**Fix:** The AI should respond helpfully regardless of prompt quality, then suggest improvements gently.

### Drop 2: Screen 6 (Portfolio)

**Emotion:** Anticipation → Disappointment
**Why:** The student expected to see the AI's response to their improved prompt but never did.
**Fix:** Show the AI's response to the improved prompt. This is the payoff.

### Drop 3: Post-Lesson

**Emotion:** Completion → Confusion
**Why:** The student isn't sure what they learned or what they can do with it.
**Fix:** Include a clear summary of what they learned and a next step.

## What's Working

### Screen 1 (Welcome)

**Emotion:** Curiosity
**Why:** The mentor greeting is friendly and sets expectations.
**Keep:** The friendly tone and clear steps.

### Screen 5 (Build)

**Emotion:** Confidence
**Why:** The checklist gives clear guidance and progress feedback.
**Keep:** The checklist pattern.

## Recommendations

### 1. Soften the First AI Response

Instead of "I need more information" and "scored 20/100", say:
"Here's what I think you're asking about. Let me try to explain AI simply..."
Then give a basic response and gently suggest improvements.

### 2. Show the Payoff

After the student writes their improved prompt, IMMEDIATELY show the AI's response to it. This is the critical moment that proves improvement works.

### 3. Celebrate Progress

When the student improves, show: "Your first attempt scored 20/100. Your improved attempt scored 80/100. That's real progress."

### 4. End with Confidence

The final screen should say: "You now know how to talk to AI effectively. This skill applies to any AI tool you use."
