**Tokenization: How AI Reads Text**
LLMs don't read words — they read tokens. "Hello, world!" becomes ["Hello", ",", " world", "!"]. This affects how AI understands meaning.

**The Attention Mechanism: What Matters**
When processing text, LLMs use attention to decide which words matter most. It's like asking: "What am I looking for?" and "What's available?"

**Context Window: How Much AI Remembers**
The context window is how much text an AI can "see" at once. GPT-4 can see 128,000 tokens — that's 96,000 words.

**Why AI Makes Mistakes**
LLMs don't "know" things — they predict tokens. This leads to hallucinations and reasoning failures. Always verify AI outputs.

**Writing Better Prompts**
Instead of "Tell me about AI," say "Explain how neural networks learn, focusing on training, backpropagation, and gradient descent." Specificity matters.
