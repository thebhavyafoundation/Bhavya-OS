## Engagement Strategy

### When someone comments

- Respond within 1 hour
- Thank them for their time
- Answer their question
- Invite them to explore more

### When someone shares

- Thank them publicly
- Ask what they found valuable
- Invite them to join the community
- Offer to answer questions

### When someone criticizes

- Acknowledge their feedback
- Ask for more details
- Thank them for helping improve
- Offer to discuss further

### When someone asks a question

- Respond within 2 hours
- Give a clear, helpful answer
- Link to relevant section in KP-001
- Invite them to ask more questions

## Crisis Management

### If there's a technical issue

- Acknowledge immediately
- Apologize sincerely
- Fix as soon as possible
- Communicate the fix

### If there's negative feedback

- Listen carefully
- Acknowledge concerns
- Respond professionally
- Take action if needed

### If there's a PR issue

- Stay calm
- Assess the situation
- Respond appropriately
- Learn from it
