# Curriculum Intelligence Engine v1.0

## Status: Discovery Phase

## Date: 2026-08-04

## Mission

The Content Factory is complete. The canonical educational unit is the Knowledge Package. Before generating the first curriculum, Bhavya Foundation must determine what deserves to be taught.

Do not generate the first 25–50 Knowledge Packages yet. First build the Curriculum Intelligence Engine.

The purpose of CIE is to continuously discover, evaluate, prioritize, and evolve the curriculum using evidence rather than intuition.

## The Question

**"If Bhavya Foundation were founded today, what should an AI engineer learn first?"**

## The Shift

**From:** "What course should we build?"
**To:** "What capabilities should a Bhavya graduate possess five years from now, regardless of which AI models or frameworks dominate?"

Design the curriculum around durable capabilities, not today's tools.

## The Problem

### What Others Do

- Teach today's tools
- Follow hype cycles
- Copy existing curricula
- Optimize for enrollment
- Measure completion

### What Bhavya Does

- Teach durable capabilities
- Follow evidence
- Design original curricula
- Optimize for transformation
- Measure impact

## The System

### Inputs

- AI research and industry trends
- Open source ecosystem
- Educational best practices
- Industry requirements
- Community feedback
- Student outcomes

### Processing

- Topic discovery
- Evidence gathering
- Scoring and ranking
- Graph construction
- Sequence optimization
- Continuous evolution

### Outputs

- Prioritized curriculum
- Learning sequences
- Knowledge Packages
- Assessment criteria
- Portfolio requirements
- Continuous updates

## Core Principles

### 1. Evidence Over Intuition

Every curriculum decision is backed by evidence, not opinion.

### 2. Capabilities Over Tools

Teach durable capabilities that outlast specific tools.

### 3. Experiential Over Theoretical

Prioritize learning by doing over learning by reading.

### 4. Continuous Over Static

The curriculum evolves with the field, not on a fixed schedule.

### 5. Mission Over Market

Curriculum serves the mission, not the market.

## Success Criteria

The CIE is complete when it can answer:

1. What are the top 100 concepts every AI engineer should learn?
2. In what order should they be learned?
3. Which concepts require projects rather than lessons?
4. Which concepts are becoming obsolete?
5. Which concepts should Bhavya Foundation monitor but not yet teach?
6. Which concepts are uniquely aligned with Bhavya Foundation's mission?

## Strategic Direction

Once CIE is complete:

- Freeze architecture
- Freeze governance
- Freeze platform expansion (except bug fixes and proven gaps)
- Spend the next major phase producing 100 exceptional Knowledge Packages

At that point, Bhavya Foundation's competitive advantage will no longer be its software — it will be its institutional knowledge, curriculum quality, and the graduates it produces.
