# Curriculum Governance

## Overview

Curriculum Governance ensures quality, consistency, and continuous improvement. Every curriculum decision follows a defined process.

## Governance Principles

### 1. Evidence-Based

Every decision is backed by evidence, not opinion.

### 2. Transparent

All decisions are documented and public.

### 3. Inclusive

All stakeholders have input.

### 4. Accountable

Clear responsibility for outcomes.

### 5. Continuous

Governance evolves with the curriculum.

## Governance Structure

### Curriculum Committee

**Role:** Strategic oversight
**Composition:**

- Curriculum Director
- Technical Lead
- Educational Lead
- Community Representative
- Student Representative

**Responsibilities:**

- Strategic direction
- Policy approval
- Quality assurance
- Resource allocation
- Performance monitoring

### Technical Review Board

**Role:** Technical quality
**Composition:**

- Technical experts
- Industry practitioners

- Research specialists

**Responsibilities:**

- Technical accuracy
- Best practices

- Technology alignment
- Quality standards

### Educational Review Board

**Role:** Educational quality
**Composition:**

- Educational experts
- Pedagogy specialists
- Assessment designers
- Accessibility experts

**Responsibilities:**

- Learning effectiveness
- Assessment quality
- Accessibility
- Inclusivity
- Educational standards

### Community Council

**Role:** Community input
**Composition:**

- Community leaders
- Student representatives

**Responsibilities:**

- Mentor feedback
- Community standards
- Community growth

## Decision Process

### Step 1: Proposal

- Define the change
- Justify the change
- Assess impact
- Document proposal

### Step 2: Review

- Technical review
- Educational review
- Community review
- AI review

### Step 3: Approval

- Committee review
- Board approval
- Community notification
- Documentation

### Step 4: Implementation

- Make changes
- Update documentation
- Notify stakeholders
- Monitor impact

### Step 5: Verification

- Measure outcomes
- Collect feedback
- Assess impact
- Document results
