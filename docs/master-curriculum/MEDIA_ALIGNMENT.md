# Media Alignment

## Overview

Every curriculum module automatically produces media assets. All media derives from the same Knowledge Packages.

## Media Types Per Module

### Blog Series

**Frequency:** 1 post per module
**Platform:** Blog, Medium, Dev.to
**Content:** Deep dive into module concepts
**Length:** 1000-2000 words
**Purpose:** SEO, education, thought leadership

### LinkedIn Carousel Series

**Frequency:** 1 carousel per module
**Platform:** LinkedIn
**Content:** Key concepts in visual format
**Length:** 10-15 slides
**Purpose:** Professional audience engagement

### YouTube Playlist

**Frequency:** 1 video per module
**Platform:** YouTube
**Content:** Video tutorial or explanation
**Length:** 10-20 minutes
**Purpose:** Visual learning, SEO

### Shorts/Reels Ideas

**Frequency:** 3-5 per module
**Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
**Content:** Quick tips, key concepts
**Length:** 30-60 seconds
**Purpose:** Quick engagement, awareness

### Newsletter Sequence

**Frequency:** 1 email per module
**Platform:** Email
**Content:** Module summary and resources
**Length:** 500-1000 words
**Purpose:** Community building, retention

### Podcast Outline

**Frequency:** 1 episode per module
**Platform:** Podcast
**Content:** Discussion of module concepts
**Length:** 30-60 minutes
**Purpose:** Audio learning, community
