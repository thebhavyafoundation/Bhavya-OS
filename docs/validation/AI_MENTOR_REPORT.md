## Strengths

### 1. Consistent Socratic Approach
All AI Mentors follow the same core principle: "Never give the answer. Ask the question that leads to the answer." This consistency is excellent.

### 2. Strong Question Sequences
Each concept has a 5-7 question sequence that leads students to discover answers. The sequences are well-designed and build on each other.

### 3. Effective Hint Systems
3-level hint systems (Gentle → More Specific → Direct) help students who are stuck without giving away answers.

### 4. Relevant Career Connections
Each AI Mentor includes 5-7 career connections that show how the skills apply to real jobs.

### 5. Misconception Handling
Guided discovery sequences for common misconceptions are well-designed and address real student beliefs.

---

## Weaknesses

### 1. No Feedback Mechanism
The AI Mentor guides describe what to ask but not how to evaluate student responses. There's no rubric for "good answer" vs. "needs improvement."

### 2. No Adaptation Logic
The guides don't address what to do if a student:
- Answers correctly on the first try
- Answers incorrectly three times in a row
- Asks a question not covered in the guide
- Shows advanced understanding

### 3. No Progress Tracking
No mechanism to track which concepts a student has mastered vs. which need more work.

### 4. No Emotional Support
The guides address cognitive struggles but not emotional ones (frustration, boredom, anxiety).

### 5. No Multi-Modal Support
The guides are text-only. No adaptation for visual, auditory, or kinesthetic learners beyond the "Alternative Approaches" section.

## Recommendations

### Critical (Must Fix)
1. **Add feedback rubrics** — Define "good answer" vs. "needs improvement" for each question sequence
2. **Add adaptation logic** — What to do if student answers correctly/incorrectly multiple times

### High Priority
3. **Add progress tracking** — Mechanism to track mastered vs. needs-work concepts
4. **Add emotional support** — Address frustration, boredom, anxiety
5. **Add multi-modal adaptation** — Different paths for visual/auditory/kinesthetic learners

### Medium Priority
6. **Add question banks** — More questions per concept for variety
7. **Add difficulty scaling** — Adjust question complexity based on student responses
8. **Add peer learning** — Suggestions for when to pair students

### Low Priority
9. **Add voice interaction** — For auditory learners
10. **Add video demonstrations** — For visual learners
11. **Add gamification** — Points, badges, streaks for motivation
