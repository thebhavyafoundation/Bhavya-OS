# Quality Rubric

## Overview

The Quality Rubric provides a weighted scoring model for evaluating Knowledge Packages. Every Knowledge Package must meet minimum thresholds to be published.

## Scoring Model

### Weights

| Area               | Weight | Description                                 |
| ------------------ | ------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Technical Accuracy | 20%    | Correctness, currency, evidence             |
| Educational Value  | 20%    | Learning effectiveness, pedagogy            |
| Hands-on Learning  | 15%    | Practice, experimentation, application      |
| AI Mentor Quality  | 10%    | Coaching, questions, hints                  |
| Portfolio Value    | 10%    | Career impact, demonstrable skills          |
| Industry Relevance | 10%    | Practical application, job market           |
| Mission Alignment  | 10%    | Builder, contributor, mentor transformation |
| Media Quality      | 5%     | Content across formats                      |

### Total: 100%

## Scoring Scale

### 5 — Excellent

- Meets all criteria
- Exceeds expectations
- Can serve as exemplar
- Minor improvements only

### 4 — Good

- Meets most criteria
- Meets expectations
- Solid quality
- Some improvements needed

### 3 — Satisfactory

- Meets basic criteria
- Acceptable quality
- Significant improvements needed
- Functional but not excellent

### 2 — Needs Improvement

- Fails to meet some criteria
- Below expectations
- Major improvements required
- Not ready for publication

### 1 — Inadequate

- Fails to meet most criteria
- Well below expectations
- Fundamental rewrite required
- Not suitable for publication

## Thresholds

### Minimum for Publication

- **Overall Score:** 3.5 average
- **No criterion below 3.0**
- **No critical issues unresolved**
- **All sections complete**
- **All reviews passed**

### Recommended for Publication

- **Overall Score:** 4.0 average
- **No criterion below 3.5**
- **All reviews passed with minor or no issues**
- **Pilot validation completed**
- **Continuous improvement plan in place**

### Exemplar Status

- **Overall Score:** 4.5 average
- **All criteria at 4.0 or above**
- **All reviews passed with no issues**
- **Pilot validation excellent**
- **Can serve as template for future KPs**

## Scoring Process

### Step 1: Individual Scoring

Each reviewer scores independently using the rubric.

### Step 2: Score Aggregation

Scores are aggregated across all reviewers.

### Step 3: Variance Analysis

Discrepancies are identified and discussed.

### Step 4: Consensus Building

Reviewers discuss and reach consensus.

### Step 5: Final Score

Final score is calculated and documented.

### Step 6: Decision

Publication decision is made based on thresholds.

## Threshold Enforcement

### Below Minimum (3.5 average or any criterion below 3.0)

- **Action:** Revision required
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks
- **Process:** Address all issues, resubmit for review

### Meets Minimum (3.5-3.9 average, no criterion below 3.0)

- **Action:** Publication with conditions
- **Timeline:** 1 week
- **Process:** Address minor issues, publish, monitor

### Meets Recommended (4.0-4.4 average, no criterion below 3.5)

- **Action:** Publication approved
- **Timeline:** Immediate
- **Process:** Publish, collect feedback, iterate

### Exemplar (4.5 average, all criteria at 4.0+)

- **Action:** Publication as exemplar
- **Timeline:** Immediate
- **Process:** Publish, use as template, share widely

## Continuous Improvement

### Score Tracking

- Track scores over time
- Identify trends
- Address patterns
- Improve process

### Threshold Adjustment

- Review thresholds quarterly
- Adjust based on evidence
- Maintain high standards
- Balance quality with productivity

### Rubric Evolution

- Update rubric based on feedback
- Add new criteria as needed
- Remove obsolete criteria
- Maintain relevance
