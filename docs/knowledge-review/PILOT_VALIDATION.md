# Pilot Validation

## Overview

Pilot Validation is the process of testing Knowledge Packages with real learners before publication. It provides evidence of effectiveness and identifies areas for improvement.

## Pilot Process

### Pre-Pilot

**Duration:** 1-2 days
**Activities:**

- Select pilot learners (5-10)
- Prepare pilot materials
- Set up tracking systems
- Establish baseline metrics
- Communicate expectations

**Criteria:**

- Learners represent target audience
- Learners committed to completing pilot
- Learners willing to provide feedback
- Learning environment ready
- Support systems in place

### Pilot Execution

**Duration:** 1-2 weeks
**Activities:**

- Deliver Knowledge Package
- Collect real-time feedback
- Monitor learning progress
- Track engagement metrics
- Document observations

**Data Collection:**

- Learning outcomes (pre/post)
- Engagement metrics
- Completion rates
- Feedback surveys
- Interview responses
- Portfolio submissions
- Reflection quality

### Post-Pilot

**Duration:** 1 week
**Activities:**

- Analyze data
- Identify patterns
- Document findings
- Create improvement plan
- Update Knowledge Package

**Deliverables:**

- Pilot report
- Improvement recommendations
- Updated Knowledge Package
- Publication decision
- Lessons learned

## Pilot Criteria

### Minimum Viable Pilot

**Requirements:**

- At least 5 learners complete
- At least 70% completion rate
- At least 3.5/5.0 satisfaction score
- At least 60% learning outcome improvement
- No major technical issues

**Duration:** 1 week

### Recommended Pilot

**Requirements:**

- At least 10 learners complete
- At least 80% completion rate
- At least 4.0/5.0 satisfaction score
- At least 70% learning outcome improvement
- Minimal technical issues

**Duration:** 2 weeks

### Exemplar Pilot

**Requirements:**

- At least 15 learners complete
- At least 90% completion rate
- At least 4.5/5.0 satisfaction score
- At least 80% learning outcome improvement
- No technical issues

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks

## Metrics

### Learning Outcomes

**Measure:** Pre/post assessment scores
**Target:** 60% improvement (minimum), 80% improvement (recommended)
**Method:** Standardized assessment before and after Knowledge Package

### Completion Rates

**Measure:** Percentage of learners who complete all activities
**Target:** 70% (minimum), 80% (recommended)
**Method:** Track activity completion in LMS

### Engagement Metrics

**Measure:** Time on task, interaction frequency, help-seeking behavior
**Target:** Appropriate time on task, moderate interaction, minimal help-seeking
**Method:** Learning analytics, user tracking

### Satisfaction Scores

**Measure:** Post-pilot survey ratings
**Target:** 3.5/5.0 (minimum), 4.0/5.0 (recommended)
**Method:** Standardized satisfaction survey

### Feedback Quality

**Measure:** Depth and specificity of feedback
**Target:** Constructive, specific, actionable feedback
**Method:** Feedback analysis, interview coding

### Portfolio Quality

**Measure:** Quality of portfolio artifacts
**Target:** Demonstrates learning, shows effort, meets expectations
**Method:** Portfolio review against rubric

## Pilot Learners

### Selection Criteria

**Requirements:**

- Represent target audience
- Diverse backgrounds and perspectives
- Willing to provide honest feedback
- Committed to completing pilot
- Available for interviews/focus groups

**Sample Size:**

- Minimum: 5 learners
- Recommended: 10 learners
- Exemplar: 15+ learners

### Incentives

**Options:**

- Free access to future Knowledge Packages

**Requirements:**

- Clear incentives before pilot
- No coercion
- Voluntary participation
- Right to withdraw

## Improvement Process

### Issue Categorization

**Critical:** Blocks learning, must be fixed
**High:** Significantly impacts learning, should be fixed
**Medium:** Moderately impacts learning, could be fixed
**Low:** Minimally impacts learning, nice to fix

### Improvement Prioritization

**Priority 1:** Critical issues (must fix before publication)
**Priority 2:** High issues (should fix before publication)
**Priority 3:** Medium issues (could fix before publication)
**Priority 4:** Low issues (fix after publication)

### Revision Process

**Step 1:** Document all issues
**Step 2:** Prioritize issues
**Step 3:** Address critical issues
**Step 4:** Address high issues
**Step 5:** Address medium issues if time permits
**Step 6:** Document low issues for future
**Step 7:** Resubmit for review
**Step 8:** Update Knowledge Package
**Step 9:** Publish revised version

## Publication Decision

### Publish Immediately

- All pilot criteria met
- No critical issues
- High satisfaction
- Strong learning outcomes

### Publish with Conditions

- Most pilot criteria met
- Minor issues to address
- Good satisfaction
- Good learning outcomes

### Revise and Repilot

- Some pilot criteria not met
- Significant issues to address
- Mixed satisfaction
- Mixed learning outcomes

### Do Not Publish

- Most pilot criteria not met
- Critical issues unresolved
- Low satisfaction
- Poor learning outcomes
