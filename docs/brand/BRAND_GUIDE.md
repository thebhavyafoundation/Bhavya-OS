# Bhavya Foundation — Brand Guide (Official)

## Brand Identity

### Tagline

**NATURE. KNOWLEDGE. HERITAGE.**

### Mission Statement

Bhavya is an institution committed to building a better tomorrow. We restore nature, advance knowledge, preserve heritage, and empower communities through ethical leadership, innovation, and public service.

### Vision

To build one of the world's most trusted public institutions advancing environmental stewardship, education, cultural preservation and responsible innovation for generations to come.

### Promise

Integrity. Excellence. Innovation. Trust.
Long-term thinking. For generations to come.

### Approach

Evidence. Impact. Sustainability.

---

## Four Pillars

### 1. Forest Mission

Restore ecosystems, increase forest cover, conserve water and protect biodiversity.

- 8+ Hectares restored

### 2. Knowledge Mission

Build AI labs, digital libraries, promote research, STEM education and digital literacy.

- 10K+ Students empowered

### 3. Heritage Mission

Document, preserve and promote our traditional knowledge, heritage, architecture and living culture.

### 4. Community Mission

Empower youth, women and communities through education, leadership, and village development.

- 50+ Communities engaged

---

## Values

1. **Integrity** — We do what is right, not what is easy
2. **Stewardship** — We care for nature, knowledge, and heritage as trustees
3. **Knowledge** — We pursue learning and truth in all we do
4. **Discipline** — We show up, do the work, and finish what we start
5. **Humility** — We listen, learn, and serve before we lead
6. **Innovation** — We solve old problems with new thinking
7. **Excellence** — We set high standards and pursue them with consistency
8. **Compassion** — We act with empathy and care for all
9. **Courage** — We speak truth, take responsibility, and act on conviction
10. **Service** — We measure success by the lives we touch

## Typography

### Headlines

- Font: **Playfair Display** or similar elegant serif
- Weight: 700 (Bold)
- Style: Uppercase for labels, Title Case for headlines

### Body

- Font: **Inter** or similar clean sans-serif
- Weight: 400-500
- Line height: 1.6

### Accent Text

- Font: **Playfair Display** or serif
- Weight: 400-500
- Style: Italic for quotes and emphasis

### Usage for Agents

When creating any Bhavya Foundation UI:

1. Reference this board for visual consistency
2. Use the exact color palette (no arbitrary colors)
3. Follow the typography hierarchy
4. Use the icon set (don't create new icons)
5. Apply the mission illustration style
6. Use the section dividers
7. Follow the glass system rules
8. Maintain editorial quality
