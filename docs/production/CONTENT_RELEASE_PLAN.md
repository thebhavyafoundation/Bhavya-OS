## 1. Overview

This document defines the release plan for the first 12 months of Production Era v1.0 (August 2026 – July 2027). It specifies curriculum levels, content milestones, freeze dates, quality review windows, and launch events.

**Goal:** Complete Levels 0-8 of the AI curriculum, producing 331 Knowledge Packages and 1,618+ educational assets.

## 8. Content Freeze Dates

### Scheduled Freezes

| Event | Freeze Period | Duration | Scope |
|-------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Q1 Review | Oct 27–31, 2026 | 1 week | No new KPs published |
| Q2 Review | Jan 26–30, 2027 | 1 week | No new KPs published |
| Q3 Review | Apr 26–30, 2027 | 1 week | No new KPs published |
| Q4 Review | Jul 27–31, 2027 | 1 week | No new KPs published |
| Annual Review | Dec 28–31, 2027 | 4 days | Full content audit |

### Freeze Activities

During content freezes:
1. **Quality Audit:** Review all published KPs for accuracy
2. **Update Cycle:** Refresh examples, verify references
3. **Gap Analysis:** Identify missing content
4. **Process Review:** Assess pipeline efficiency
5. **Planning:** Finalize next quarter's content plan

### Emergency Freeze Protocol

If a critical quality issue is detected:
1. Immediately halt all new publications
2. Quarantine affected KPs
3. Conduct root cause analysis
4. Fix and re-validate affected content
5. Resume publication after Founder approval

| Event | Scope | Owner | Duration |
|-------|-------|-------|----------|

| Q2 Review | Full KP audit | Production Lead + Founder | 4 hours |
| Q3 Review | Full KP audit | Production Lead + Founder | 4 hours |
| Q4 Review | Full KP audit + annual planning | Production Lead + Founder | 8 hours |

### Quality Review Checklist

- [ ] All KPs pass 10 quality gates
- [ ] No KP has quality score below 3.5
- [ ] All examples are verifiable and current
- [ ] All references resolve to real sources
- [ ] No plagiarism detected
- [ ] Indian context represented (min 1 example per KP)
- [ ] Age-appropriate content for target grade
- [ ] Learning outcomes measurable (Bloom's taxonomy)
- [ ] Exercises test understanding, not memorization
- [ ] Prerequisites link to existing KPs

## 11. Risk Management

### Content Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| KP quality below threshold | Medium | High | 10 quality gates, human review |
| Content becomes outdated | Medium | Medium | Quarterly review cycle |
| Insufficient examples | Low | Medium | Indian context requirement |
| Plagiarism detected | Low | High | Automated plagiarism check |
| Technical inaccuracy | Medium | High | Technical review gate |

### Schedule Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| KP production delayed | Medium | Medium | Buffer time allocation |
| Review bottleneck | Medium | Medium | Rotating reviewer roles |
| Founder unavailability | Low | High | Delegate approval authority |
| Holiday disruptions | High | Low | Advance scheduling |

### Resource Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
