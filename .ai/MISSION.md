# Bhavya Foundation — Mission

**Motto:** Restoring Nature. Empowering Humanity. Preserving Heritage.

---

## Purpose

Bhavya Foundation exists to:

1. **Restore and conserve forests**, biodiversity, and natural ecosystems
2. **Promote environmental awareness** and sustainable practices
3. **Expand equitable access** to education, digital literacy, and responsible AI
4. **Establish digital libraries**, learning centers, and research initiatives
5. **Preserve cultural heritage**, traditional knowledge, and community wellbeing
6. **Empower youth, women, and rural communities** through education and skill development
7. **Encourage research, innovation, and technology** that advance the public good

---

## Missions

### 1. Bhavya Forest Mission

Restore degraded lands using native Himalayan species. Target: 10,000 hectares by 2030.

### 2. Bhavya Knowledge Mission

Build the world's first AI-powered digital library accessible to every learner.

### 3. Bhavya Heritage Mission

Document and preserve India's intangible cultural heritage through AI.

### 4. Bhavya Community Mission

Empower 100 rural communities through technology and education.
