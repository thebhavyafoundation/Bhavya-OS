PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation was established from the conviction that enduring institutions are built through service, integrity, knowledge, environmental stewardship, and respect for cultural heritage.
The office of Founder exists not to confer privilege, ownership, or permanent control, but to preserve the original vision of the Foundation and to guide its early development with responsibility, humility, and accountability.
This Charter defines the role of the Founder, establishes the principles of Founder's Stewardship, and records the expectations that future generations may understand the purpose for which Bhavya Foundation was established.

ARTICLE 1
THE OFFICE OF THE FOUNDER
The Founder of Bhavya Foundation is:
Shri Manohar Lal
The title Founder shall remain permanently associated with the individual who established the Foundation.
The title shall not be inherited, transferred, sold, assigned, or otherwise assumed by any future individual.
The Founder is recognized as the originator of the Foundation's vision, philosophy, and institutional identity.

ARTICLE 2
THE PURPOSE OF THE FOUNDER
The Founder exists to protect the Foundation's purpose.
The Founder is expected to:
inspire long-term thinking;
preserve institutional values;
encourage ethical leadership;
strengthen governance;
mentor future leaders;
protect public trust;
ensure that the Foundation remains faithful to its constitutional principles.
The Founder shall never regard the Foundation as personal property.

ARTICLE 3
THE PRINCIPLE OF STEWARDSHIP
The Founder accepts stewardship rather than ownership.
Stewardship means acting as a temporary guardian of a permanent institution.
The Founder shall therefore:
protect rather than possess;
build rather than control;
empower rather than dominate;
preserve rather than exploit;
serve rather than rule.
The Founder shall lead by example.

ARTICLE 4
FOUNDATIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES
The Founder shall strive to:
Protect Nature
Support ecological restoration, forest conservation, biodiversity, water conservation, and environmental education.

Expand Knowledge
Promote AI education, digital literacy, research, open learning, and innovation for public benefit.

Preserve Heritage
Support lawful conservation of heritage, traditional knowledge, yoga, and community culture.

Build Institutions
Encourage the creation of enduring institutions that continue serving society beyond the lifetime of any individual.

Strengthen Communities
Support initiatives that increase local leadership, volunteerism, education, and self-reliance.

ARTICLE 5
LEADERSHIP PHILOSOPHY
The Founder believes that leadership is measured not by authority, but by responsibility.
A leader should:
listen before deciding;
learn before teaching;
serve before expecting service;
protect before expanding;
build systems instead of dependence;
prepare successors rather than create followers.
Leadership shall be exercised with humility.

ARTICLE 6
ETHICAL COMMITMENTS
The Founder commits to:
honesty;
lawful conduct;
transparency;
accountability;
respect for every individual;
responsible stewardship of Foundation resources;
avoidance of conflicts of interest;
continuous learning.
The Founder shall willingly remain subject to the Constitution and governance framework of the Foundation.

ARTICLE 7
RELATIONSHIP WITH THE BOARD
The Founder shall work collaboratively with the Board of Trustees.
The Founder shall encourage independent thinking, respectful debate, and informed decision-making.
The Board shall not exist merely to endorse the Founder's views.
Likewise, the Founder shall respect the fiduciary responsibilities of the Board.
The relationship between Founder and Board shall be guided by trust, openness, and shared commitment to the Foundation's mission.

ARTICLE 8
DECISION-MAKING
The Founder shall seek to make decisions based upon:
evidence;
long-term impact;
public benefit;
constitutional principles;
financial responsibility;
environmental sustainability;
institutional integrity.
Short-term popularity shall never outweigh long-term public interest.

ARTICLE 9
BUILDING LEADERS
One of the Founder's greatest responsibilities is to develop future leaders.
The Founder shall encourage:
education;
mentorship;
independent thinking;
ethical conduct;
professional competence;
public service.
The success of the Foundation shall ultimately depend upon institutions rather than personalities.

ARTICLE 10
PUBLIC CONDUCT
The Founder represents Bhavya Foundation both publicly and privately.
Accordingly, the Founder shall strive to:
communicate truthfully;
respect diversity;
avoid conduct that brings the Foundation into disrepute;
maintain dignity in public discourse;
protect public confidence.

ARTICLE 11
PERSONAL COMMITMENT
The Founder acknowledges that:
the Foundation does not exist to advance personal wealth, political influence, or individual recognition.
Its purpose is to create lasting public benefit.
The Founder accepts that future generations may improve the Foundation in ways not yet imagined.
Such progress should be welcomed when it remains faithful to the Foundation's constitutional values.

ARTICLE 12
THE FOUNDER'S PLEDGE
I establish Bhavya Foundation with the intention of serving society through environmental stewardship, education, heritage preservation, and community development.
I accept the responsibility of Founder & Steward with humility.
I shall strive to protect the Foundation's mission, uphold its Constitution, respect the law, encourage ethical leadership, and safeguard the trust placed in this institution.
I recognize that the Foundation belongs to its charitable purpose and the communities it serves.
May every generation entrusted with its leadership strengthen this institution with wisdom, integrity, and compassion.

Constitutional Appendix
Founder's Guiding Principles
Every major decision should answer five questions:
Does it protect Nature?
Does it expand Knowledge?
Does it preserve Heritage?
Does it create measurable public benefit?
Will future generations thank us for this decision?
If the answer to these questions is "yes," the Foundation is likely acting consistently with its mission.

Founder’s Closing Statement
"I do not wish to build an organization that depends on me. I wish to help build an institution that remains worthy of public trust long after I am gone."
