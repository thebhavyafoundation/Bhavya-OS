PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation exists to serve the public through environmental conservation, education, heritage preservation, and community development.
Financial support from individuals, institutions, corporations, philanthropic organizations, and governments enables the Foundation to fulfill its charitable mission.
This Policy establishes the principles governing the acceptance, management, acknowledgement, and refusal of donations to ensure that every contribution strengthens, rather than compromises, the Foundation's integrity and independence.

ARTICLE 1
PURPOSE
This Policy aims to:
Protect the independence of the Foundation.
Maintain public trust.
Ensure lawful acceptance of donations.
Respect donor intent where appropriate.
Prevent inappropriate influence over governance.
Promote ethical fundraising.
Ensure transparency and accountability.

ARTICLE 2
GUIDING PRINCIPLES
Every donation accepted by Bhavya Foundation shall satisfy the following principles:
Public Benefit
The donation must advance the charitable mission of the Foundation.

Integrity
The donation shall not compromise the Foundation's ethical standards or independence.

Legality
The donation shall comply with all applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

Transparency
The acceptance, recording, and use of donations shall be documented accurately.

Stewardship
Every donation shall be managed responsibly and used for its intended charitable purpose.


ARTICLE 5
RESTRICTED AND UNRESTRICTED DONATIONS
Unrestricted Donations
Unless otherwise agreed, donations shall be treated as unrestricted and may be applied wherever the Foundation determines they are most needed in furtherance of its charitable objectives.

Restricted Donations
The Foundation may accept donations designated for a specific programme, project, or purpose, provided that:
the restriction is lawful;
it aligns with the Foundation's mission; and
the Foundation has the capacity to administer the funds responsibly.
If the specified purpose later becomes impossible, impracticable, or unlawful, the Foundation shall manage the funds in accordance with applicable law and any relevant donor agreement.

ARTICLE 6
DONATIONS THE FOUNDATION MAY DECLINE
The Foundation may decline any donation that:
conflicts with its Constitution or charitable purposes;
is reasonably believed to arise from unlawful activities;
would compromise the Foundation's independence;
imposes conditions inconsistent with applicable law or the Foundation's mission;
creates an unacceptable conflict of interest;
poses a significant reputational risk; or
cannot be administered responsibly.
The Board shall retain the final authority regarding significant or unusual donations.

ARTICLE 7
DONOR INFLUENCE
Donors shall not receive governance rights by virtue of making a donation.
Specifically, donations shall not automatically entitle a donor to:
appointment of Trustees;
control over Board decisions;
influence over recruitment;
ownership of Foundation assets;
operational control; or
preferential treatment inconsistent with the Foundation's charitable purposes.
The Foundation values partnership but shall preserve its institutional independence.

ARTICLE 8
NAMING RIGHTS
The Foundation may, in appropriate circumstances, recognize significant contributions through acknowledgements or naming of facilities, programmes, or initiatives.
Any such recognition shall:
be consistent with the Foundation's values;
be approved by the Board;
not imply ownership or governance rights; and
remain subject to review if circumstances materially change.

ARTICLE 9
ANONYMOUS DONATIONS
The Foundation may accept anonymous donations where permitted by law.
Anonymous donations shall be handled with appropriate financial controls and recorded in accordance with legal requirements.
Where the source of funds cannot be reasonably verified or raises concerns regarding legality, the Foundation may decline the donation.

ARTICLE 10
DONOR PRIVACY
The Foundation shall respect the privacy of donors.
Personal information shall be collected, stored, and used in accordance with the Foundation's Data Privacy Policy and applicable law.
Donor information shall not be disclosed without lawful authority or the donor's consent, except where required by law.

ARTICLE 11
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF DONATIONS
The Foundation shall issue appropriate acknowledgements or receipts for donations received.
Acknowledgements should include:
receipt number;
date;
donor name (where available);
amount or description of donation;
purpose (if restricted); and
authorized signature or digital authentication.

ARTICLE 12
ENDOWMENTS
The Foundation may establish endowment funds to support its long-term charitable mission.
The terms governing each endowment shall be documented in writing and approved by the Board.
Endowment assets shall be managed prudently and in accordance with applicable law.

ARTICLE 13
CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY (CSR)
The Foundation may collaborate with companies under CSR initiatives where eligible and where such collaborations:
align with the Foundation's mission;
comply with applicable laws;
preserve the Foundation's independence; and
are governed by written agreements where appropriate.
CSR partnerships shall not permit corporate influence over the Foundation's governance.

ARTICLE 14
INTERNATIONAL CONTRIBUTIONS
The Foundation may receive contributions from outside India only in compliance with applicable laws governing foreign contributions.
No foreign contribution shall be accepted unless all required legal approvals and registrations are in place.

ARTICLE 15
FUNDRAISING PRACTICES
All fundraising activities shall be:
truthful;
transparent;
respectful;
lawful; and
consistent with the Foundation's values.
The Foundation shall not knowingly make misleading claims or exert improper pressure on potential donors.

ARTICLE 16
USE OF DONATIONS
Donations shall be applied efficiently and responsibly to further the Foundation's charitable purposes.
The Foundation shall maintain records demonstrating the use of significant donations and grants.
Where appropriate, donors may receive reports regarding the utilization of restricted funds.

ARTICLE 17
REVIEW AND COMPLIANCE
This Policy shall be reviewed at least every two years or earlier where:
legal requirements change;
fundraising practices evolve; or
the Board determines revision is appropriate.
Compliance with this Policy shall be monitored through the Foundation's governance and financial oversight processes.

APPENDIX A
Donation Acceptance Checklist
Before accepting a significant donation, consider:
☐ Does the donation support our mission?
☐ Is it lawful?
☐ Could it compromise our independence?
☐ Have any conflicts of interest been disclosed?
☐ Can we comply with any lawful restrictions attached to the donation?
☐ Would accepting the donation strengthen or weaken public trust?

Closing Statement
"A donation is more than a financial contribution. It is an expression of trust. Bhavya Foundation shall honour that trust by accepting support ethically, administering it responsibly, and applying it faithfully to the public good."
Founder’s Principle on Giving
"We shall never pursue funding at the cost of our principles. Resources can be rebuilt; trust, once lost, is difficult to restore. Every contribution accepted by Bhavya Foundation must strengthen both our mission and our integrity."
