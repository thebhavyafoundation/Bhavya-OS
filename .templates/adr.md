# Template: ADR (Architecture Decision Record)

## Metadata

```yaml
title: "[ADR Title]"
date: "[Decision Date]"
status: "[proposed/accepted/rejected/superseded]"
deciders: "[Decision Makers]"
```

## Structure

### Status

- Current status
- Date of status change

### Context

- Business context
- Technical context
- Constraints

### Decision

- What was decided
- Why this decision

### Consequences

- Positive consequences
- Negative consequences
- Risks

### Alternatives Considered

- Alternative 1
- Alternative 2
- Why alternatives were rejected

### References

- Related ADRs
- External references
- Documentation

## ADR Style

- Concise
- Factual
- Well-reasoned
- Documented
