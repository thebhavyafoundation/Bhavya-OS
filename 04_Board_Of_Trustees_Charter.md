PREAMBLE
The Board of Trustees is entrusted with the highest responsibility within Bhavya Foundation.
The Board does not exist to manage personalities, pursue private interests, or administer authority for its own sake.
The Board exists to safeguard the Foundation's charitable mission, uphold public trust, ensure responsible stewardship of resources, and preserve the institution for future generations.
Every Trustee accepts this office voluntarily and acknowledges that service on the Board is a public responsibility requiring integrity, diligence, independence, and sound judgment.

ARTICLE 1
PURPOSE OF THE BOARD
The Board exists to:
Protect the Constitution.
Protect the Trust.
Protect the Mission.
Protect Public Trust.
Ensure Financial Integrity.
Ensure Legal Compliance.
Guide Long-Term Strategy.
Appoint Leadership.
Supervise Institutional Performance.
Preserve the Foundation for future generations.

ARTICLE 2
ROLE OF THE BOARD
The Board shall:
Govern.
Not manage.
The Board establishes direction.
Management executes.
Trustees oversee.
Staff implement.
Volunteers support.
This separation protects both governance and operational efficiency.

ARTICLE 3
THE DUTY OF A TRUSTEE
Every Trustee owes five constitutional duties.

Duty One
Duty of Loyalty
The interests of Bhavya Foundation come before:
personal interests;
family interests;
political interests;
business interests; and
organizational interests.

Duty Two
Duty of Care
Every Trustee shall:
Prepare before meetings.
Read reports.
Ask questions.
Exercise independent judgment.
Make informed decisions.

Duty Three
Duty of Integrity
Every Trustee shall:
Act honestly.
Avoid corruption.
Avoid favoritism.
Avoid misuse of Foundation resources.
Protect confidentiality.
Maintain dignity.

Duty Four
Duty of Stewardship
Every Trustee shall leave the Foundation stronger than they found it.

Duty Five
Duty to Future Generations
Every decision should consider:
"What impact will this have ten years from now?"


ARTICLE 8
DECISION MAKING
Consensus should always be preferred.
Where voting becomes necessary:
Every Trustee has one vote unless the Trust Deed provides otherwise.
Dissenting opinions should be recorded where appropriate.
Constructive disagreement strengthens governance.

ARTICLE 9
CONFLICT OF INTEREST
A Trustee shall disclose any personal, financial, familial, or professional interest that may influence—or reasonably appear to influence—their judgment.
Where a material conflict exists, the Trustee shall not participate in the discussion or decision unless permitted under the Foundation's Conflict of Interest Policy and applicable law.
All disclosures shall be recorded in the minutes.

ARTICLE 10
CONFIDENTIALITY
Trustees shall protect:
Personal information.
Donor information.
Financial information.
Legal matters.
Personnel matters.
Strategic discussions.
Confidentiality survives resignation from the Board unless disclosure is required by law.

ARTICLE 11

The Board shall encourage:
Respect.
Independent thinking.
Evidence-based decisions.
Healthy debate.
Continuous learning.
Professionalism.
Trust.
The Board shall reject:
Fear.
Politics.
Favoritism.
Personal agendas.
Complacency.

ARTICLE 12
COMMITTEES
The Board may establish:
Finance & Audit Committee
Governance & Ethics Committee
Nature Committee
Knowledge Committee
Heritage Committee
Risk Committee
Technology Committee
Fundraising Committee
Each committee shall operate under written terms of reference approved by the Board.

ARTICLE 13
BOARD EVALUATION
At least once every two years, the Board should review its own effectiveness, including:
attendance;
preparedness;
quality of decision-making;
governance practices;
strategic oversight; and
implementation of previous resolutions.
The purpose of evaluation is institutional improvement rather than personal criticism.

ARTICLE 14
REMOVAL AND VACANCIES
Vacancies and removal of Trustees shall be handled in accordance with:
the Trust Deed;
the Constitution;
applicable law; and
principles of procedural fairness.
No Trustee shall be removed arbitrarily.

ARTICLE 15
OATH OF A TRUSTEE
Before assuming office every Trustee shall affirm:
"I accept this office freely and solemnly. I shall faithfully uphold the Constitution and Trust Deed of Bhavya Foundation. I shall act honestly, independently, and in the public interest. I shall protect the Foundation's mission, preserve its integrity, safeguard its resources, avoid conflicts of interest, and discharge my responsibilities with diligence, humility, and accountability."

ARTICLE 16
BOARD COMMITMENT
The Board recognizes that its authority exists solely to advance the charitable purposes of Bhavya Foundation.
The Board shall strive to govern with wisdom, integrity, transparency, and respect for the communities served by the Foundation.

