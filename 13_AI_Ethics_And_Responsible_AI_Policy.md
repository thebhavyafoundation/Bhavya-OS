BHAVYA FOUNDATION
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ETHICS & RESPONSIBLE AI POLICY
Policy Number: BF-AI-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Founding Edition
Approved By: Board of Trustees
Review Cycle: Every 2 Years

PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation recognizes Artificial Intelligence (AI) as one of the defining technologies of the twenty-first century.
The Foundation believes that AI should enhance human capability, expand access to knowledge, strengthen communities, and promote the public good.
AI shall be developed, taught, deployed, and used responsibly, transparently, and lawfully.
Technology shall always remain accountable to human values.
The Foundation rejects the use of AI for unlawful surveillance, discrimination, deception, exploitation, or harm.

ARTICLE 1
PURPOSE
This Policy establishes the ethical principles governing all AI-related activities undertaken by Bhavya Foundation, including:
AI Education
AI Labs
Digital Libraries
Research
Community Outreach
Software Development
Automation
Digital Platforms
Internal Operations

ARTICLE 2
GUIDING PRINCIPLES
Every AI activity shall uphold:
Human Dignity
Transparency
Fairness
Safety
Privacy
Accountability
Inclusivity
Public Benefit
Environmental Responsibility
Continuous Learning

ARTICLE 3
HUMAN-CENTRIC AI
AI shall assist human decision-making rather than replace human responsibility.
The Foundation shall encourage AI systems that:
support learning;
improve productivity;
increase accessibility;
expand opportunity; and
empower communities.
Critical decisions affecting individuals should include appropriate human oversight.

ARTICLE 4
AI EDUCATION
Through the Bhavya Knowledge Mission, the Foundation may establish AI education programmes that include:
AI literacy
Prompt engineering
Machine learning fundamentals
Responsible AI
Robotics
Programming
Data science
Open-source AI tools
Digital entrepreneurship
AI for agriculture
AI for education
AI for public service
The objective is to make AI education accessible to rural and underserved communities.

ARTICLE 5
BHAVYA AI LABS
The Foundation may establish Bhavya AI Labs as centres for learning, experimentation, and innovation.
AI Labs may provide:
computers and workstations;
internet-enabled learning spaces;
open-source AI software;
coding workshops;
innovation challenges;
mentorship;
research support;
career guidance; and
community hackathons.
AI Labs shall prioritize learning over commercialization.

ARTICLE 6
RESPONSIBLE USE OF AI
Representatives of the Foundation shall not knowingly use AI to:
generate deceptive or fraudulent content;
impersonate individuals without authorization;
facilitate unlawful activities;
promote hatred or violence;
infringe intellectual property rights; or
intentionally spread misinformation.
AI outputs should be reviewed before being relied upon for significant decisions or public communications.

ARTICLE 7
FAIRNESS & NON-DISCRIMINATION
The Foundation shall strive to ensure that AI-related activities do not unfairly disadvantage individuals or communities.
Educational programmes should promote awareness of bias, fairness, and the limitations of AI systems.

ARTICLE 8
PRIVACY & DATA
AI systems used by the Foundation shall respect applicable privacy laws and the Foundation's Data Privacy Policy.
Personal data shall only be collected and processed where necessary, lawful, and proportionate.
Sensitive information shall receive additional protection.

ARTICLE 9
TRANSPARENCY
Where the Foundation uses AI in educational, administrative, or public-facing contexts, it should be transparent about the role of AI where appropriate.
Participants should understand when AI is being used as a learning tool or decision-support system.

ARTICLE 10
RESEARCH & OPEN KNOWLEDGE
The Foundation encourages ethical AI research and the creation of educational resources that expand public understanding.
Where appropriate, the Foundation should support open educational resources, open-source technologies, and collaborative learning.
Research involving people or personal data shall be conducted ethically and in accordance with applicable laws.

ARTICLE 11
DIGITAL INCLUSION
The Foundation shall strive to reduce the digital divide by:
establishing AI learning centres in rural communities;
supporting affordable access to technology;
providing training to first-generation learners;
encouraging participation by women, youth, and underserved groups; and
promoting accessible educational materials.

ARTICLE 12
ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY
The Foundation recognizes that AI technologies consume energy and computing resources.
Where practical, the Foundation shall:
encourage efficient computing practices;
extend the useful life of equipment;
promote responsible e-waste management;
consider environmental impacts when deploying technology.
Innovation should support both people and the planet.

ARTICLE 13
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Participants shall respect copyright, licensing terms, trademarks, patents, and other intellectual property rights.
The Foundation shall encourage lawful use of open-source software and responsible attribution of creative works.

ARTICLE 14
AI GOVERNANCE
The Board may establish an AI Ethics & Technology Committee to:
review significant AI initiatives;
monitor emerging developments;
recommend updates to this Policy;
encourage ethical innovation; and
advise on technology-related risks.

ARTICLE 15
TRAINING
Individuals involved in AI programmes should receive training on:
responsible AI;
privacy;
cybersecurity;
digital ethics;
intellectual property;
misinformation awareness; and
safe use of AI tools.
Learning shall be continuous.

ARTICLE 16
POLICY REVIEW
This Policy shall be reviewed every two years or earlier where:
AI technology changes significantly;
legal requirements evolve; or
the Board determines revision is appropriate.

APPENDIX A
AI Ethics Checklist
Before deploying an AI system, ask:
☐ Does it serve a genuine public benefit?
☐ Does it respect privacy?
☐ Could it unfairly disadvantage anyone?
☐ Is there meaningful human oversight?
☐ Are users informed appropriately?
☐ Is the technology lawful and secure?
☐ Have potential risks been considered?

APPENDIX B
AI Lab Participant Pledge
"I will use Artificial Intelligence responsibly, ethically, and lawfully. I will respect the rights and dignity of others, protect privacy, verify information before relying upon it, and use technology to expand knowledge, solve meaningful problems, and contribute positively to society."

APPENDIX C
AI Lab Standards
Every Bhavya AI Lab should strive to provide:
Safe and inclusive learning environments.
Open-source software where practical.
High-speed internet access.
Modern computing equipment.
Structured AI curriculum.
Mentorship.
Career guidance.
Community innovation programmes.
Accessibility for learners from diverse backgrounds.

Closing Declaration
"Artificial Intelligence is not merely a technological advancement. It is a societal responsibility. Bhavya Foundation shall promote AI that expands human potential, strengthens communities, protects individual rights, and serves the public good."

Founder's AI Principle
"Technology should never replace wisdom, compassion, or human judgment. Our mission is not to create more users of Artificial Intelligence, but to cultivate responsible creators, innovators, and leaders who use AI to solve real problems, uplift communities, and preserve human dignity."
