PREAMBLE
Bhavya Foundation is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity, impartiality, and public trust.
Individuals serving the Foundation must act solely in the best interests of the Foundation and the communities it serves.
This Policy establishes procedures to identify, disclose, manage, and, where necessary, eliminate conflicts of interest.
The purpose of this Policy is not to prevent relationships or outside interests, but to ensure that such interests do not improperly influence Foundation decisions.

ARTICLE 1
PURPOSE
This Policy seeks to:
Protect the Foundation's integrity.
Maintain public confidence.
Promote transparency.
Ensure fair decision-making.
Protect Trustees and office-bearers from allegations of misconduct.
Comply with applicable laws and good governance practices.

ARTICLE 2
APPLICABILITY
This Policy applies to:
Founder & Steward
Managing Trustee
Trustees
Committee Members
Advisors
Employees
Volunteers (where relevant)
Consultants
Contractors involved in Foundation decisions
Every covered person shall comply with this Policy.

ARTICLE 3
GUIDING PRINCIPLES
All decisions shall be:
Honest.
Independent.
Fair.
Transparent.
Free from improper influence.
Focused on public benefit.
No individual shall place personal interests above the interests of the Foundation.

ARTICLE 4
WHAT IS A CONFLICT OF INTEREST?
A conflict of interest exists where an individual's personal, financial, professional, family, or other private interest could reasonably be expected to influence—or appear to influence—their ability to act objectively in the interests of the Foundation.
Both actual conflicts and perceived conflicts shall be taken seriously.

ARTICLE 5
TYPES OF CONFLICTS
5.1 Financial Conflict
Examples:
Owning a business seeking a Foundation contract.
Receiving commissions or undisclosed financial benefits.
Personal investments affected by Foundation decisions.

5.2 Family Conflict
Examples:
Hiring close relatives.
Awarding contracts to family businesses.
Supervising immediate family members without safeguards.
Family relationships do not automatically prohibit involvement, but they require disclosure and appropriate management.

5.3 Professional Conflict
Examples:
Serving another organization whose interests materially conflict with the Foundation.
Holding advisory roles that may influence Foundation decisions.

5.4 Personal Relationships
Close personal relationships that may affect impartial decision-making shall be disclosed where relevant.

5.5 Gifts and Hospitality
Accepting gifts, hospitality, travel, or benefits that may influence—or appear to influence—Foundation decisions constitutes a potential conflict.

ARTICLE 6
DUTY TO DISCLOSE
Every covered person shall disclose a conflict:
upon appointment;
annually through a written declaration;
immediately when a new conflict arises; and
before participating in any relevant discussion or decision.
Failure to disclose may itself constitute a breach of this Policy.

ARTICLE 7
REGISTER OF INTERESTS
The Foundation shall maintain a confidential Register of Interests.
The Register may include:
employment;
directorships;
business ownership;
significant shareholdings;
family relationships relevant to Foundation decisions;
consulting engagements;
recurring gifts or hospitality requiring disclosure.
The Register shall be maintained by the Secretary and reviewed periodically.

ARTICLE 8
MANAGEMENT OF CONFLICTS
When a material conflict exists, appropriate measures may include:
disclosure to the Board;
recusal from discussion;
abstention from voting;
reassignment of decision-making;
independent review; or
other safeguards approved by the Board.
The chosen approach shall be proportionate to the nature and significance of the conflict.

ARTICLE 9
PROCUREMENT SAFEGUARDS
Where a Trustee, employee, or related party has an interest in a procurement process:
the interest shall be disclosed in writing;
the individual shall not participate in evaluation or approval;
competitive procedures should be followed where appropriate;
the decision and safeguards shall be documented.
Related-party transactions shall receive heightened scrutiny.

ARTICLE 10
RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTIONS
Transactions involving Trustees, officers, employees, or their close relatives shall be permitted only where:
they are lawful;
they demonstrably serve the Foundation's interests;
the terms are fair and reasonable;
the conflict has been disclosed;
the conflicted individual does not participate in the approval process; and
the transaction is approved in accordance with governance procedures.

ARTICLE 11
GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
Representatives shall not solicit gifts or personal benefits.
Modest ceremonial gifts may be accepted where customary and lawful, provided they do not influence decision-making and are handled consistently with the Foundation's Gift Policy.
Where uncertainty exists, the matter should be referred to the Governance & Ethics Committee.

ARTICLE 12
ANNUAL DECLARATION
Every Trustee and senior office-bearer shall complete an Annual Conflict of Interest Declaration confirming:
current interests;
new interests since the previous declaration; and
compliance with this Policy.

ARTICLE 13
INVESTIGATION AND ENFORCEMENT
Credible concerns regarding conflicts of interest shall be reviewed fairly and promptly.
Any person subject to an allegation shall have an opportunity to respond before decisions are made.
Where a breach is established, appropriate corrective measures may include additional disclosures, recusal, policy training, disciplinary action, or other measures consistent with the Constitution, governance documents, and applicable law.

ARTICLE 14
CONFIDENTIALITY
Information disclosed under this Policy shall be handled confidentially, except where disclosure is required:
by law;
for governance purposes; or
to protect the Foundation's legitimate interests.

ARTICLE 15
RESPONSIBILITY OF THE BOARD
The Board shall:
promote a culture of transparency;
review significant disclosures;
ensure this Policy is implemented consistently; and
periodically review the effectiveness of conflict management procedures.

ARTICLE 16
REVIEW
This Policy shall ordinarily be reviewed every two years or earlier where:
significant legal changes occur;
governance needs evolve; or
the Board determines revision is appropriate.

APPENDIX A
Conflict of Interest Decision Checklist
Before participating in a decision, ask:
Do I, or someone close to me, stand to benefit from this decision?
Could a reasonable person question my impartiality?
Have I fully disclosed all relevant interests?
Should I withdraw from this discussion or vote?
Would I be comfortable if this situation were publicly reported?
If the answer to any question raises concern, disclose the matter and seek guidance before proceeding.
