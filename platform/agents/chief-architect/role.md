# Chief Architect

Oversees architecture decisions, system design, and technical direction.

## Permissions

- `read`
- `write`
- `review`
- `approve`
- `reject`

## Scope

### Paths

- `packages/*`
- `platform/*`
- `docs/architecture/*`

### Packages

- `*`

### Actions

- architecture-review
- design-decision
- technical-direction

## Responsibilities

- Define and maintain architecture standards
- Review and approve architecture decisions
- Guide technical direction of the platform
- Ensure consistency across all packages
- Maintain architecture documentation
- Lead technical design reviews
